# Gap audit

## Proved in this cumulative archive

1. The exact latent ordered-block identity `F=G+E`, with legal common-shift local directions and fixed latent weights.
2. The independent complete-law information budget for `E`.
3. The full-moving-law degree cancellation and fixed-interior response estimate, including explicit constants.
4. The exact negative-covariance two-bit odds payment in Lemma 5.1 of `proof.md`.
5. An exact rational rank-three projection violating the atomwise total payment
   \[
   2\sum_{i<j}h_{ij}\le\sum_i f_i
   \]
   by more than `6/5`.
6. A growing direct-sum family in dimensions `6k` whose pointwise defect exceeds `6k/5`.

## Disproved

1. Coordinate submodularity, by the inherited exact two-bit high-contrast example.
2. The inherited single-site completion estimate (C).
3. The inherited pair-by-pair conditional and all-outside-pattern-averaged Fisher allocations.
4. The new stronger atomwise **total** once-only Fisher payment, by Proposition 6.1.

## Diagnostic only

1. Small-dimensional searches of the actual ordered conditioned leaf sum found no positive `G''` for the tested Fourier roots.
2. Fixed-weight midpoint relative-entropy curvature was nonnegative in the tested samples.
3. Several apparent universal midpoint conditional-entropy inequalities failed under direct optimization for arbitrary laws; those failed candidates are recorded in `attempts.md`.

None of these computations certifies a sign theorem.

## Unresolved main link

For `m=floor(sqrt n)`, fixed positive density, high contrast, and a fixed nonshrinking interior interval, prove

\[
\sup_a\sum_j\mathbb E_u
F_{(Q_n^u)_{B_j}}''(a,c)\le o(n),
\]

or prove the corresponding finite concavity-deficit estimate. The actual distribution of the conditional kernels must be retained.

## Statements not disproved

- averaged conditioned leaf-volume concavity;
- S-sine entropy-rate concavity;
- an expected, atom-averaged total Fisher allocation different from the pointwise rule tested here.

## Endpoint link

Once an interior finite Jensen estimate with `o(n)` error is proved, finite entropy continuity and the coupling bound `|H(a)-H(b)|<=n b(|a-b|)` extend it to `a=0,1-c`. No twice differentiability of the limiting rate is needed. This link is ready but currently has no volume estimate to apply to.

## Exact scope check for the new obstruction

A separate all-atom rational-log certificate proves that the complete entropy Hessian of the six-dimensional projection example is below `-1000`. Thus the growing bad-atom family is an obstruction to the proposed pointwise budget only; the base example itself has strongly negative averaged curvature.
