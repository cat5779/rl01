# Claim and gap audit

## What is closed in the author argument

1. The reference pair is computed in the actual half-density Fourier
   operator, at c=19/20 throughout a in [1/50,3/100]. The scalar margin is
   certified on a nonzero complete perturbation box, not just at grid nodes.
2. The deterministic finite-volume argument pays both the arbitrary far
   observation changes and the Toeplitz compression boundary. It never
   replaces a finite Toeplitz compression by a projection.
3. The event used in probability estimates is a fixed **finite** cylinder.
   Its true channel likelihood is bounded below before summing the latent
   and remaining output variables. All translations are summed by linearity,
   without a block-product model.
4. The positive defect is bounded below at lag 5 in all large volumes and
   every exact S7 local table of sufficiently large radius. The cutoff is
   fixed independently of n, a, R and the larger observation radius.
5. Every load-bearing computational input is generated by the delivered
   standard-library checker. All rational comparisons are active under
   `python -O`; the full 200-cell tables are deterministically regenerable.

## What is not closed, and must not be inferred

- The full high-contrast sine entropy-rate target is not settled.
- The signed sum of g is not shown positive or negative. A positive expected
  positive part does not show a positive expectation of the signed defect.
- The theorem only disproves an o(n) / vanishing-exception repair of the
  existing gamma<=2 two-budget comparison. An explicitly paid nonzero
  exception allowance, signed grouping, or a different budget remains live.
- No numerical value of K_(R,L) with R,L large enough to pay S7's unchanged
  constants is certified. Its negative margin remains unpaid in this work.
- The Fisher-refund comparison is only a failure of refund-alone against
  the **published allowance**, not evidence that the actual observation
  error has that sign or size.
- Parity decimation does not remove outer word-weight derivatives. Its
  cross and acceleration terms are explicitly left unpaid.
- The enormous lower-bound denominator is not hidden. The result is
  asymptotic nonvanishing, not an effective sampling frequency or a large
  negative/positive entropy-rate margin.
- Floating finite n<=20 signs and n<=128 alternating-word diagnostics do not
  enter the proof of the entropy-rate target or the infinite reference.
