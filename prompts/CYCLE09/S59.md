## Reviewed conditional-table objects

For i!=0, condition on all Y_j except j=0,i and let
p_ab^(i)=Pr(Y_0=a,Y_i=b | Y_(Z minus {0,i})). These are normalized actual
conditional probabilities, with their actual exterior law mu_c^(-0,-i).
Write, on the disjoint union of those exterior spaces,

ell_i=log[p_10 p_01/(p_00 p_11)] >=0,
Delta_i=p_10 p_01-p_00 p_11 >=0,
E_i=Delta_i sum_(a,b) 1/p_ab,
U_i=p_00+p_11,
d nu_c(i,z)=b E_i(z) d mu_c^(-0,-i)(z).

Let q=Pr(Y_0=1 | all other outputs), D=q(1-q), and
r(p)=ell / {(1-exp(-ell))[1+(exp(ell)-1)U]}.
Use its continuous value r=1 at ell=0, where E_i=0 anyway. The reviewed
identities and inequalities are

J(c)=sum_(i!=0) E ell_i,
nu_c(all)=1-b E(1/D),
c^2/(1+c^2) <= nu_c(all) <= c^2,
b J(c)=integral r dnu_c,
b Gamma_c(0)=1-integral r dnu_c.

All infinite expectations and pair sums here have been paid at fixed gap.

Toeplitz compressions have leakage and are NOT projections.
