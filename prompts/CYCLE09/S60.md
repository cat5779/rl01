## Frozen universal question

Let K be any finite complex Hermitian strict contraction, 0<K<I, and let
v be a fixed complex vector. For any real a with 0<K+aI<I, let

M_y(a)=K+aI-diag(1-y), V_y(a)=M_y(a)^(-1),
p_a(y)=(-1)^(n-|y|) det M_y(a),
F_v(K+aI)=sum_(y in {0,1}^n) p_a(y) [v* V_y(a) v]^2.

This is the actual full-configuration Fisher information for the rank-one
direction t vv*, evaluated along a common diagonal shift a. The rank-one
direction v is fixed as a varies. All strict kernels have positive atoms.

Determine whether, for every such n,K,v,a,

partial_a^2 F_v(K+aI) >=0.                       [CF]

Do not silently replace the common direction I by independent coordinate

If false, provide a small explicit strict contraction and v with an exact

candidate is not the final counterexample. A valid counterexample solves
the universal question even if it does not refute any sine-model theorem.

At any fixed a, abbreviate V=V_Y(a), and
s=Tr V, u=v*Vv, z=v*V^2v, w=v*V^3v. The full moving-law identity is

F_v''=E[(s^2-Tr V^2)u^2 -4suz +2z^2 +4uw].

The probability acceleration and score terms are essential. For independent
diagonal coordinates, W=Vv, d_i=V_ii and z_i=|W_i|^2 give

(F_v)_ii=2 E |W_i|^4 >=0,
(F_v)_ij=E[(d_i d_j-|V_ij|^2)u^2

## Why CF would matter, without overstating its consequences

Let H(K) denote classical full-configuration Shannon entropy and define
Phi(K)=partial_a^2 H(K+aI)|_(a=0). Along a rank-one line K+t vv*, every
full atom is affine in t. Therefore

partial_t^2 H(K+t vv*)=-F_v(K+t vv*),
partial_t^2 Phi(K+t vv*)=-partial_a^2 F_v(K+t vv*+aI)|_(a=0).

If CF is true, Phi is concave on each legal rank-one segment. For a genuine
one-site DPP reveal, block kernel A, external probability q and coupling v,

A_1=A-vv*/q, A_0=A+vv*/(1-q),
q A_1+(1-q)A_0=A.

When only the block's common diagonal offset varies, q and v stay fixed.
CF would imply
q Phi(A_1)+(1-q)Phi(A_0) <= Phi(A),
and hence nonnegative mutual-information curvature in that block-only shift.
