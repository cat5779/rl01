## Frozen actual model

All logarithms are natural. Let n=2k tend to infinity through even integers,
c=19/20, a_*=(1-c)/2=1/40, and xi_*=1/1520. On sites 0,...,n-1 set

P_st = n^(-1) sum_{r=0}^{k-1} exp(2*pi*i*r*(s-t)/n).

Draw a k-subset A with probability det(P_A). Conditional on A, independently
draw Y_i with Pr(Y_i=1|A)=a+c*1_{i in A}. Let pi_l(a)=Pr(|Y|=l),
q_l(a)=Law(Y ||Y|=l), and u_l be uniform on l-subsets. Thus

sum_l pi_l(a)t^l = [1-a-c+(a+c)t]^k [1-a+at]^k.

At a_*, the full output is DPP(a_*I+cP). Equivalently its L-ensemble matrix
is Lambda=39P+(I-P)/39, so q_l(S) is det(Lambda_S) normalized over l-subsets.
Do not substitute a radial overlap law, count entropy, spectral entropy or an
arbitrary balanced projection for this full spatial law.

Here is the fixed corrected law; it may not be redesigned to fit a proof.
On l-subsets let C_l=l(n-l) and let the Johnson generator have total rate one:

(L_l h)(S)=C_l^(-1) sum_{i in S,j notin S}[h(S-i+j)-h(S)].

For l<=k let r_l^max(S)=binom(n,l)det(P_S)/binom(k,l), a density relative
to u_l; use complementation for l>=k. This is the same true prior of A,
followed by uniformly choosing a subset (or a superset), not a uniform prior.
Set m=min(l,n-l), d=k-m+1, xi=a(1-c-a)/c, and

Q_r^(d)(xi)=sum_{s=0}^r
  [r_falling_s*(r+2d-1)_rising_s/(d_rising_s*s!)] xi^s,
gamma_2,l=2(n-1)/C_l,
tau_l=-log(Q_(m-2)^(d)/Q_m^(d))/gamma_2,l,
g_l=exp(tau_l L_l)r_l^max,    qhat_l=u_l g_l.

This defines m>=2; at m<=1 use the uniform law. Evaluate at xi_* unless
a derivative is explicitly written. Actual and corrected laws share the

## Exact conditional object and target

For 3<=m<k put p=q_(m+1), phat=qhat_(m+1). Uniform deletion K_m maps
probability laws on (m+1)-subsets to laws on m-subsets:

(K_m p)(S)=1/(m+1) sum_{x notin S} p(S+x).

Set P_m=K_m p, Phat_m=K_m phat and

alpha_p(x|S)=p(S+x)/[(m+1)P_m(S)],
alpha_phat(x|S)=phat(S+x)/[(m+1)Phat_m(S)],
C_m=E_(S~P_m) D(alpha_p(.|S)||alpha_phat(.|S)) >=0.

The conditioning observes the COMPLETE retained configuration S. It does
not observe the latent input A. A bound conditional on A must be transferred
legally through the latent mixture, with all information terms accounted for.
Do not confuse probability deletion 1/(m+1) with density deletion 1/(n-m).

Let alpha=(1-c^2)/4, beta=(1+c^2)/2 and
p_0(t)=alpha(1+t^2)+beta*t. Define B_(n,r) as the coefficients of

B_n(t)=2k p_0(t)^(k-1)+k(k-1)(1+t)^2 p_0(t)^(k-2).

Let omega_(n,0)=B_(n,0), omega_(n,m)=B_(n,m)-B_(n,m-1) for 1<=m<k.
These weights are nonnegative and sum_{m<k} omega_(n,m)=O(n^(3/2)).

Your primary sufficient target is the asymptotic statement

             sum_{m=3}^{k-1} omega_(n,m) C_m = o(n).                 (T)

All constants may depend on the fixed c, but not n or an unrestricted layer
index. A uniform C_m<=K/n would suffice and give O(sqrt(n)), but is STRONGER
than needed and is not granted. A central-layer bound plus a fully paid tail,
or an actual flux-weighted estimate, is equally acceptable. Retain m=3 and
the edge m=k-1; vanishing interpolation terms do not make C_3 vanish.

W_rel := sum_l pi_l''(a_*)R_l(a_*)
       = -2 sum_{m=3}^{k-1} omega_(n,m)[C_m+eta_m R_(m,xi)+J_m].
