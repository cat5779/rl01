## Exact mathematical object

Fix c=19/20 first; a theorem on a larger high-contrast interval is welcome.
Let n=2k tend to infinity through even integers. On n cyclic sites put
P_st=(1/n) sum_(r=0)^(k-1) exp(2 pi i r(s-t)/n).
The latent k-subset A has law nu(A)=det P_A.

Let u_l be the uniform law on l-subsets.
If l<=k, sample S uniformly among l-subsets of A.
If l>=k, sample S uniformly among l-subsets containing A.
Averaging over nu gives q_l^max; write r_l^max=q_l^max/u_l.
Use the UNNORMALIZED Bernoulli–Laplace generator
(G_l f)(S)=sum_(i in S,j notin S) [f(S-i+j)-f(S)].

Put a_*=(1-c)/2, b=(1-c^2)/4 and z=((1+c)/(1-c))^2.
For 2<=l<=n-2, m=min(l,n-l), define
Z_m(z)=[t^m](1+t)^k(1+zt)^k,
alpha_m=m(m-1)/(k(k-1)),
theta_(n,l)(z)=(z-1)^2 [t^(m-2)](1+t)^(k-2)(1+zt)^(k-2)
                  /(alpha_m Z_m(z)),
s_l=-log(theta_(n,l)(z))/(2(n-1)).
The corrected probability law is
mu_l(S)=u_l(S) [exp(s_l G_l) r_l^max](S).
At l=0,1,n-1,n set mu_l=u_l.

The actual spatial cyclic output at the midpoint is
Q_(n,c)=DPP((1-c)I/2+cP).
Its count-l conditional law is exactly
gamma_(l,c)(S)=det([I+(z-1)P]_S)/Z_l(z).
Both laws are uniform on the four endpoint layers.

Define, with natural logarithms,
U_(n,l)(c)=D(mu_l || gamma_(l,c)).
The actual count weights at general a are given by
sum_l pi_l(a,c)t^l
 =[1-a-c+(a+c)t]^k [1-a+at]^k.
Let kappa_l=partial_a^2 pi_l(a,c) evaluated at a=a_*.
The derivatives here act only on pi: U_(n,l)(c) is frozen at the midpoint.
Your object is
Psi_n(c)=(1/n) sum_(l=0)^n kappa_l U_(n,l)(c).
