in K_x(h)=hI+x(Q-I/2). For a finite exterior J around anchor 0, let
P_(h,1)^J=Law(Y_J|Y_0=1), P_(h,0)^J=Law(Y_J|Y_0=0),
d_h^J=P_(h,1)^J-P_(h,0)^J,
p_(h,alpha)^J=alpha P_(h,1)^J+(1-alpha)P_(h,0)^J, 0<=alpha<=1.
Define the Pearson–Fisher variational functional
I_h^J(alpha)=sum_z (d_h^J(z))^2/p_(h,alpha)^J(z)

Its optimizer is f_*^J=d_h^J/p_(h,alpha)^J.
