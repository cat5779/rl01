## Fixed law and source frontier

and Q_(n,c)=DPP((1-c)I/2+cP) in the spatial basis. Set b=(1-c^2)/4.
For its actual outputs define r_i=Pr(Y_i=1|Y_-i), x_i=2r_i-1,
h_i=log(r_i/(1-r_i)). For i!=j, partial_j h_i denotes the Boolean difference
h_i(Y_-{i,j},Y_j=1)-h_i(Y_-{i,j},Y_j=0). The expectation is over the actual
remaining marginal; these are not freely chosen pair tables. Define
R_n(c)=sum_(i!=j) E partial_j h_i+n c^2/b.

The source derives the exact actual-law Stein response

|x_i|<=c^2 only gives
(V_n-nc^6)/(b(1-c^4)) <= R_n <= V_n/b,

You must retain how a conditional susceptibility is distributed along the
