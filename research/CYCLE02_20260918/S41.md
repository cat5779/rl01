## 2. Entropy production is nonincreasing in original-rate time

On the \(l\)-slice,

\[
G_l=\sum_{1\le i<j\le n}(T_{ij}-I).
\]

Pairs whose two coordinates have equal occupation contribute zero, while each occupied-empty pair gives one Bernoulli–Laplace swap. Thus this is exactly \(l(n-l)L_l\), not a rescaled approximation.

For a positive density \(f\),

\[
\mathcal I_l^G(f)
=
\frac12\sum_{i<j}
\mathbb E_{u_l}
\Psi(f,T_{ij}f),
\]

where

\[
\Psi(x,y)=(x-y)(\log x-\log y).
\]

Its Hessian is

\[
\nabla^2\Psi(x,y)=
\begin{pmatrix}
(x+y)/x^2&-(1/x+1/y)\\
-(1/x+1/y)&(x+y)/y^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The diagonal entries are positive and the determinant is zero, so the Hessian is positive semidefinite. Hence \(\Psi\) is jointly convex.

The sum of all transpositions is invariant under coordinate permutations, so \(e^{tG_l}\) commutes with each \(T_{ij}\). Joint Jensen therefore gives, pointwise,

\[
\Psi(e^{tG_l}f,e^{tG_l}T_{ij}f)
\le
e^{tG_l}\Psi(f,T_{ij}f).
\]

After integration against invariant \(u_l\),

\[
\mathcal I_l^G(e^{tG_l}f)
\le
\mathcal I_l^G(f).
\]

Consequently \(s\mapsto\mathcal I_l^G(s)\) is nonincreasing. citeturn263833view1

### Repair of a minor text gap at zero densities

The needed bound nevertheless follows rigorously. For any \(0<\varepsilon<s\), the density at time \(\varepsilon\) is strictly positive, and monotonicity on positive times gives

\[
(s-\varepsilon)\mathcal I_l^G(s)
\le
\int_\varepsilon^s\mathcal I_l^G(t)\,dt
=
F_l(\varepsilon)-F_l(s).
\]

Finite-state entropy is continuous under the semigroup, so \(F_l(\varepsilon)\to F_l(0)\). Letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) gives

\[
s\mathcal I_l^G(s)
\le
F_l(0)-F_l(s)
\le
F_l(0).
\]

Thus, at the corrected clock,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal I_l^G(s_l)
\le
\frac{F_l(0)}{s_l}
=
\frac{2(n-1)F_l(0)}
{-\log\theta_{n,l}}.

This is a **text gap, not a false theorem**.

## 3. Initial entropy on every layer

First suppose \(l=m\le k\). Given a projection-DPP input set \(A\), the maximal-overlap rule selects \(S\) uniformly among the \(m\)-subsets of \(A\). Therefore

\[
q_m^{\max}(S)
=
\frac{\Pr(S\subseteq A)}{\binom{k}{m}}
=
\frac{\det P_S}{\binom{k}{m}}.
\]

The inclusion identity follows from Cauchy–Binet, as indicated in the submission. Since the cyclic rank-\(k\) projection has

\[
P_{ii}=\frac12,
\]

Hadamard's inequality gives

\[
\det P_S\le\prod_{i\in S}P_{ii}=2^{-m}.
\]

Relative to the uniform \(m\)-slice law,

\[
r_m^{\max}(S)
=
\binom{2k}{m}
\frac{\det P_S}{\binom{k}{m}}
\le
\frac{\binom{2k}{m}}
{2^m\binom{k}{m}}.
\]

Now

\[
\frac{\binom{2k}{m}}{\binom{k}{m}}
=
\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}\frac{2k-j}{k-j}.
\]

The logarithms of these factors increase with \(j\). Hence their first-\(m\) average is at most their first-\(k\) average:

\[
\frac1m\log\frac{\binom{2k}{m}}{\binom{k}{m}}
\le
\frac1k\log\binom{2k}{k}
\le\log4.
\]

Thus

\[
\frac{\binom{2k}{m}}{\binom{k}{m}}\le4^m,
\qquad
\|r_m^{\max}\|_\infty\le2^m.
\]

It follows that

\[
\boxed{
F_m(0)=D(q_m^{\max}\|u_m)
\le\log\|r_m^{\max}\|_\infty
\le m\log2.
}
\]

These are the bounds used by the submission. citeturn263833view2

## 4. First coefficient-ratio argument: upper bound on the sensitivity

Write

\[
Z_m(z)=\sum_j
\binom{k}{j}\binom{k}{m-j}z^j
\]

and

\[
A_{m-2}(z)=
\sum_j
\binom{k-2}{j}
\binom{k-2}{m-2-j}z^j.
\]

Then

\[
\theta_m(z)
=
\frac{(z-1)^2A_{m-2}(z)}
{\alpha_mZ_m(z)},
\qquad
\alpha_m=\frac{m(m-1)}{k(k-1)}.
\]

Let \(J_Z,J_A\) have distributions proportional to the summands of \(Z_m,A_{m-2}\). Then

\[
\partial_z\log\theta_m
=
\frac2{z-1}
+
\frac{\mathbb E J_A-\mathbb E J_Z}{z}.
\]

For corresponding coefficients,

\[
w_j:=
\frac{[z^j]A_{m-2}}{[z^j]Z_m}
=
\frac{(k-j)(k-j-1)(m-j)(m-j-1)}
{k^2(k-1)^2}.
\]

For \(0\le j\le m-2\), all four numerator factors decrease with \(j\); for \(j=m-1,m\), \(w_j=0\). Hence \(w_j\) is nonincreasing over the entire support.

The \(A\)-law is the \(Z\)-law tilted by \(w\), so

\[
\mathbb E J_A-\mathbb E J_Z
=
\frac{\operatorname{Cov}_Z(J,w_J)}
{\mathbb E_Zw_J}
\le0.
\]

The covariance sign follows, for example, from

\[
2\operatorname{Cov}(J,w_J)
=
\mathbb E[(J-J')(w_J-w_{J'})]\le0
\]

for an independent copy \(J'\).

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\partial_z\log\theta_m(z)
\le\frac2{z-1}.
}
\]

The coefficient ratio and stochastic-order direction are correct. citeturn263833view3

## 5. Second coefficient-ratio argument: positivity of the sensitivity

\[

\sum_{r=0}^m c_rq_ry^r,

where

\[

\frac{r(r-1)(2k-m)(2k-m-1)}
{k(k-1)(2k-r)(2k-r-1)}.

Here \(q_0=q_1=0\). For \(r\ge2\),

\[
\frac{q_{r+1}}{q_r}

\frac{(r+1)(2k-r)}
{(r-1)(2k-r-2)}

whenever \(r<m\). Thus \(q_r\) is nondecreasing and nonconstant. Also

\[
q_m=\frac{m(m-1)}{k(k-1)}=\alpha_m.

\[
\theta_m=\frac{\mathbb E q_R}{\alpha_m}

and differentiation with respect to \(\log y\) gives

\[
y\partial_y\theta_m
=
\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(R,q_R)}{\alpha_m}

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\partial_z\log\theta_m(z)\ge0.

\[
0<\theta_m(z)<1

for every finite \(z>1\). Thus the corrected clock is strictly positive on every interior layer. citeturn263833view3turn263833view4
