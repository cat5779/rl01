# Status

The frozen nonexistence claim (NS0) is **disproved**. At the fixed kernel \(K=I/2\), the canonical flow
\[
\Phi_E(P)(S,i)=2^{-(|E|-1)}P_{ii}
\]
is a Borel, coordinate-permutation-equivariant positive selector with dimension-free Lipschitz constant \(1\).

This does not construct a selector for general kernels. It is compatible with failure of full-fiber Hausdorff stability because a selector need not choose the unstable circulating flows.
