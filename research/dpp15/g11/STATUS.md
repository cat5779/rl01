PROVED; independent mathematical review: PASS.

For every fixed finite support bound (s), the unique least-Euclidean-norm point of the full positive capacitated birth-flow fiber is a Borel, coordinate-permutation-equivariant selector with a finite Lipschitz constant (C_{\varepsilon,s}) independent of the ambient dimension. The constant is not uniform in (s); the unrestricted arbitrary-support theorem remains open. No novelty claim is made.
