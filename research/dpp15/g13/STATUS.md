# Status

PROVED; independent mathematical review: PASS.

A global invariant prescribed-generator weak pure-birth law exists if and only if invariant dynamically admissible endpoint laws exist independently on every cell along some sequence of partitions whose mesh tends to zero. The proof closes disintegration, pasting, compact birth-time interpolation, fixed-marginal L1 martingale closure, time zero, path support, and no-simultaneous-positive-jump issues. It does not prove that the local invariant endpoint laws exist for arbitrary groups. No novelty claim is made.
