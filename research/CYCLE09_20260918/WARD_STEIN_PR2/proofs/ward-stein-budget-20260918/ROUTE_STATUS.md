# Ward--Stein budget route: cross-repository status

**Conclusion first: LIVE_AVERAGED_ROUTE / POINTWISE_CLOSURE_REFUTED / NOT_A_THRESHOLD_IMPROVEMENT.**

The Ward--Stein construction in \`RESULT.md\` is **not** an already-refuted proof route in its surviving form. What is already refuted, both elsewhere in the DPP repositories and inside this packet, is the stronger strategy of closing the proof by a universal pointwise or layerwise inequality. The only live continuation is to exploit the **actual complete-output average and sine/projection spatial geometry** in

\[
\Gamma(c)=\frac{4}{1-c^2}\left(1-\mathbb E_{\rm sine,c}\mathcal B_c\right).
\]

As a positivity theorem this packet is weaker than the accepted universal baseline: it proves only \(0<c\le\sqrt3/2\approx0.8660\), whereas the repository baseline is \(0\le c\le37/40=0.925\). It therefore must not be advertised as an improved contrast threshold. Its value, if the new identities survive independent review, is structural: it replaces the V14 signed local kernel by one explicit nonnegative complete-law budget whose expectation alone controls the sign.

**Evidence status:** the packet is an author proof with replayed author-side exact arithmetic. It has not received fresh independent mathematical review.

## 1. Comparison with routes already proved to fail

### A. Separate pointwise pair bounds / the universal two-budget quotient

Current \`dpp-entropy-concavity\` records that positive conditioned pair contributions occur and that separate pointwise suprema destroy the compatibility between output atoms, odds ratios and completion weights. The refined two-budget local criterion is certified through \(37/40\), but an exact feasible witness at \(c=463/500\) violates even the local constant needed to continue that closure.

This is a genuine warning for the present packet. Its scalar envelope \(\mathcal B_c(z)\le b(c^2)\) is another universal pointwise closure. It stops well before the accepted \(37/40\) theorem and therefore cannot be the high-contrast mechanism.

Moreover this packet contains its own stronger obstruction: an exact 32-coordinate half-rank projection word at \(c=19/20\) satisfies \(\mathcal B_c(z)>1.17\). Thus the tempting rule \(\mathcal B_c(z)\le1\) for every projection and every word is false.

**Disposition:** pointwise closure is REFUTED; the expected budget \(\mathbb E\mathcal B_c<1\) is not refuted.

The Ward--Stein identity does not assume pointwise resolvent positivity. Its edge coefficient is a covariance/log-odds chord averaged under the true complete output law. Therefore PR124/127 does not refute the surviving identity; it reinforces the requirement not to split that expectation into independently signed resolvent pieces.

Hence any interpretation of the new budget as a proof by nonnegative count layers is already impossible. The Ward--Stein formula does **not** use such a decomposition: the expectation is taken over the complete physical word law after all edge terms are assembled.

### E. Direct displacement / entropic-interpolation flux

The same S9 candidate ledger checked the natural discrete-displacement analogy and found a nonzero BB residual \(W^2(BC-AD)\); the canonical source flux therefore does not satisfy the desired equality. Importantly, that ledger explicitly states that a **different flux/reference is not ruled out**.

### F. Earlier Poisson-corrector work

The tools repository already contains Poisson/RPF correctors, notably PR115 and its successor line. PR115 was superseded because its own finite Neumann/sign gate was unfinished; it was not closed by a theorem saying that all Poisson corrections are impossible. That line concerns a specialized finite-memory two-harmonic true-rate problem and an invariant-state response.

The present object is different: its claimed potential is an explicit linear magnetization for a finite complete-output generator of the balanced projection channel. Therefore the generic idea "use a Poisson corrector" is not new to the project, but the present identity is not rendered false by PR115's disposition. No novelty claim is made here.

## 2. Relation to the accepted 37/40 baseline

The accepted universal theorem proves common-diagonal-channel concavity for every finite Hermitian positive contraction, and hence stationary entropy-rate concavity, through \(c=37/40\). The present one-variable envelope reaches only \(\sqrt3/2<37/40\).

Therefore:

1. **Theorem strength:** worse than the existing baseline.
2. **Method status:** not fully refuted.
3. **High-contrast value:** entirely conditional on obtaining a genuinely averaged, sine-specific estimate
   \[
   \mathbb E_{\rm sine,c}\mathcal B_c<1
   \]
   beyond \(37/40\). Re-optimizing the universal pointwise envelope alone is not a credible continuation.
4. **At \(c=19/20\):** the packet currently has no sign. Its universal envelope is above one and the general-projection pointwise closure is explicitly false.

## 3. Why the surviving interface matches the repository's stated open problem

The current synthesis says that, beyond the pointwise two-budget boundary, a successful proof must retain averaging across signed contributions, complete-output orbits, spatial minor geometry, or some equivalent global compatibility. The reviewed S9 work likewise leaves the **complete subleading signed aggregate** as the critical gap.

If correct, the Ward--Stein identity is useful precisely because it packages that aggregate into one expectation under the actual complete law. But it has not yet used enough sine-specific spatial/minor geometry to prove the expectation below one at high contrast.

The smallest credible next theorem is therefore not another universal chord majorant. It is a model-specific inequality for the true sine posterior environment, for example a correlation/transport estimate showing a strict average saving relative to the worst-word envelope.
