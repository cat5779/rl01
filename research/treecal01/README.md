# A star-calibrated quotient lift with a wrong distant-pair law

There is a parameter in (1137/1000, 229/200) for which the critical Bernoulli-cluster quotient construction matches every single-vertex star of the unit WUSF, yet has a different distant-pair cylinder. In fact the vacancy of the outer edges of a three-edge path exceeds 5/48 throughout that interval.

Read [STATEMENT.md](STATEMENT.md), then the complete [THEOREM.md](THEOREM.md) and [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md). This is a reviewed computer-assisted limitation of one specified construction. It does not exclude parameters outside the interval or other conditional constructions, and it does not settle the invariant or joint iid endpoint problem.
