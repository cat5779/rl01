## 2. Exact actual-law allocation interface

Let \(V\) be finite and

\[
K_V(a)=aI+\frac{19}{20}Q_{1/3,V}.
\]

Because \(0\preceq Q_{1/3,V}\preceq I\) and \(0<a<1/20\), this is a strict
finite DPP.  Write \(p_a(y)\) for its full configuration mass.  Introduce
independent diagonal shifts \(a_i\), and define the coordinate scores

\[
Z_i(y)=\partial_{a_i}\log p(y),\qquad
J_V=E\left(\sum_i Z_i\right)^2,
\qquad F_V=\sum_i E Z_i^2.\tag{2.1}
\]

The determinant differentiation identities give

\[
E Z_iZ_j=E|G_{ij}|^2\quad(i\ne j),
\qquad
J_V=F_V+\sum_{i\ne j}E|G_{ij}|^2.\tag{2.2}
\]

For a conditional two-site table \((A,B,C,D)=(p_{00},p_{10},p_{01},p_{11})\),
put

\[
s=BC-AD,
\qquad
\alpha(A,B,C,D)=
\frac{\log(BC/AD)}{s(A^{-1}+B^{-1}+C^{-1}+D^{-1})},\tag{2.3}
\]

with continuous value one at independence.  The exact entropy Hessian can be
written as the sum of conditional pair log-odds terms minus \(J_V\).  Hence a
global conditional-table envelope \(\alpha\le C(a)\), together with guarded
improvements

\[
\alpha_{i,i+1}\le c_z(a)=C(a)-\delta_z(a),\tag{2.4}
\]

gives

\[
H_V''\le (C-1)J_V-CF_V
       -2\sum_e E\{\delta_e Z_iZ_j\}.\tag{2.5}
\]

The last identity is an actual-law identity: the guard coefficient excludes
the two target bits, so conditional normalization converts
\(E[\delta_e|G_{ij}|^2]\) to \(E[\delta_e Z_iZ_j]\).

For every selected nearest-neighbour edge set

\[
B_z(a)=
\begin{pmatrix}
C(a)/2&\delta_z(a)\\
\delta_z(a)&C(a)/2
\end{pmatrix}.\tag{2.6}
\]

If \(0\le\delta_z\le C/2\), then \(B_z\succeq0\).  Translating consecutive
edges gives each interior site exactly two incident diagonal allocations of
\(C/2\), hence exactly \(C\); boundary sites receive less.  No diagonal
budget is spent twice.

Let \(A_e\) be a local window containing the target pair and guard.  The score
martingale

\[
E[Z_{V,e}\mid Y_{A_e}]=Z_{A_e,e}\tag{2.7}
\]

and conditional PSD Jensen localize (2.5).  For any fixed local two-vector
function \(f\), completing the square and using the multi-affine integration
by parts identity

\[
E[Z_i g(Y)]=E[\Delta_i g(Y)]\tag{2.8}
\]

give the computable local payment

\[
P_f(a)=E_a V_f(a,Y),\qquad
V_f=2\sum_{i\in e}\Delta_i(B_zf)_i-f^{\mathsf T}B_zf.\tag{2.9}
\]

Crucially, \(E_a\) in (2.9) is the actual law at \(a\), even when \(f\) is
chosen at another parameter.

The common-shift Fisher information obeys the complete-data bound

\[
J_V\le |V|d(a),\qquad
d(a)=\frac{2/3}{a(1-a)}+
     \frac{1/3}{(a+19/20)(1/20-a)}.\tag{2.10}
\]

Equations (2.5)--(2.10) are the only analytic interface used by the finite
certificate.

## 3. Cap-retuning lemma

### Lemma 3.1 — legal affine cap retuning

Fix guard caps \(c_z(a)\) and a local test \(f\).  Suppose an affine function
\(\widetilde C(a)\) satisfies, on a compact interval \(L\),

\[
\alpha_{ij}\le\widetilde C(a)\quad\hbox{for every conditional pair},
\qquad
\frac{\widetilde C(a)}2\le c_z(a)\le\widetilde C(a)\quad\hbox{for every }z.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Set \(\widetilde\delta_z=\widetilde C-c_z\), and form (2.6) with
\(\widetilde C\).  Then the allocation and variational conclusions
(2.5)--(2.9) remain valid.  In particular, if

\[
P_f(a)-(\widetilde C(a)-1)d(a)\ge\eta,
\qquad P_f(a)\le M,\tag{3.2}
\]

for an \(m\)-site stationary witness, then for every \(n\ge m\),

\[
H_n''(a)\le-\eta n+(m-1)M.\tag{3.3}
\]

**Proof.**  The first inequality in (3.1) repeats the global reduction to
(2.5).  The second is exactly
\(0\le\widetilde\delta_z\le\widetilde C/2\), so each edge matrix is PSD.
Each site is incident to at most two translated nearest-neighbour witnesses,
and their diagonal entries are \(\widetilde C/2\), so the pointwise total is
at most \(\widetilde C\).  Conditional score Jensen and (2.8) give (2.9).
There are \(n-m+1\) translated windows, so

\[
H_n''\le n(\widetilde C-1)d-(n-m+1)P_f
       \le-\eta n+(m-1)M.
\]

No derivative of an approximation or of a value error is taken. \(\square\)

For S69, the retuned cap is (1.1).  It is lower than the old S63 cap over the
new interval.  This reduction lowers the extensive burden more than it lowers
the actual-law variational payment, which repairs the lower endpoint.

## 9. A reusable three-site frame for the remaining upper gap

The following algebraic lemma is proved here for future use.  It is not needed
for (T69), and no unproved distance-two guard is imported into the theorem.

### Lemma 9.1 — nearest plus distance-two frame without double spending

Suppose a legal global cap \(C\) supplies nearest-neighbour improvements
\(\delta_i\) and distance-two improvements \(\gamma_i\), each measurable in
a guard excluding its pair endpoints.  For every consecutive triple define

\[
\mathcal B_i=
\begin{pmatrix}
q&\delta_i/2&\gamma_i\\
\delta_i/2&q&\delta_{i+1}/2\\
\gamma_i&\delta_{i+1}/2&q
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad q=C/3.\tag{9.1}
\]

If every \(\mathcal B_i\succeq0\) pointwise, then the translated frame uses
exactly the available diagonal budget, exactly the nearest improvements, and
exactly the distance-two improvements:

* every site belongs to three triples, contributing \(3q=C\);
* every nearest edge belongs to two triples, contributing
  \(\delta_i/2+\delta_i/2=\delta_i\);
* every distance-two edge belongs to one triple, contributing \(\gamma_i\).

Therefore the PSD allocation/conditional-score-Jensen proof applies to the
sum of the triple quadratics without spending any diagonal term twice.

For direct checking, (9.1) is PSD iff all principal minors are nonnegative;
in addition to the three two-by-two minors, the determinant condition is

\[
q^3-q\left(\frac{\delta_i^2}{4}
+\frac{\delta_{i+1}^2}{4}+\gamma_i^2\right)
+\frac{\delta_i\delta_{i+1}\gamma_i}{2}\ge0.\tag{9.2}
\]

**Proof.**  Sum the matrices embedded in the full coordinate space.  The
three coefficient-counting identities above give the exact diagonal and
off-diagonal coefficients.  Endpoint exclusion permits the actual-law pair
identity for every allocated off-diagonal term.  PSD permits conditional
score Jensen on the union of the triple and its guards.  The determinant in
(9.2) is the direct determinant expansion. \(\square\)

This frame is a concrete route around (8.4): it can add a second edge class
using spectral slack in a three-site block rather than attaching another
independent pair matrix and reusing the same diagonal budget.

## 10. Falsification attempts

1. **Old lower endpoint.**  Exact rational evaluation of the inherited S63
   polynomial shows an upper gap below \(-0.05922669\) at \(a=.02\); the old
   fixed witness genuinely cannot certify that endpoint.
2. **Sample interpolation.**  No endpoint is accepted from sampled signs.
   Global and guard bounds are continuum interval certificates, and payment
   is closed by exact Bernstein coefficients.
3. **Moving-reference derivative.**  Replacing \(p_a\) by the anchor law was
   tested and rejected.  The final calculation evolves the entire probability
   vector by the nilpotent common-shift operator and includes \(V_1\).
4. **Rare words.**  No probability cutoff is used.  Every one of the
   4,194,304 configurations has a positive lower enclosure.
5. **Upper test optimization.**  At \(a=.026\), the exact score-matched
   ceiling is still negative by more than \(0.0963\), so changing the local
   test alone is not a plausible repair.
6. **Meaning of the obstruction.**  The negative sufficient-witness ceiling
   is not asserted to be a negative entropy curvature or a counterexample to
   concavity.
