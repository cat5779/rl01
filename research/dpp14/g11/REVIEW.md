- the original positive capacitated flow fiber is nonempty;
- the least-Euclidean-norm point of a polyhedron with fixed inequality normals is globally Lipschitz in the right-hand side, including degenerate active sets;
- for cube dimension at most six, the integer Gram determinant argument gives a uniform finite Hoffman constant;
- conditioning on inactive coordinates gives the exact Cartesian product decomposition of the entire fiber;
- the least-norm selector therefore factors compatibly across every containing support set;
- comparing two inputs only uses the union of their supports, of size at most six;
