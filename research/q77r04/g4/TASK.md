# G4: decide a concrete nonunimodular Hecke DPP test

Frozen target: Gamma=BS(2,3)=<a,t | t a^2 t^(-1)=a^3>, H=<a>, W=Gamma/H with left multiplication. Define the bounded Hecke operator by T delta_(gH)=sum_(h in H/(H intersect tHt^(-1))) delta_(gh tH). This is independent of the chosen representative gH. Put A=T+T*, rho=||A||, and Q*=I/2+A/(2 rho). Prove or disprove that the determinantal law P^(Q*) is a Gamma-equivariant measurable factor of iid Uniform[0,1] labels indexed by W. This is one explicit W-source problem, not a claim that every nonunimodular DPP is a factor. Do not replace W-indexed labels by Gamma-indexed labels.

First verify the coset convention, T, positivity of Q*, and rho. Relate the graph to the Bass--Serre tree and derive any spectral radius formula used. At the root the subdegrees should be 3 and 2 (verify); thus the root functional tau(B)=<B delta_H,delta_H> is not a trace: tau(T*T)=3 and tau(TT*)=2. This obstructs a particular tracial proof; it is NOT evidence that the DPP itself fails to be a factor.

Try a genuinely suitable mechanism: the oriented tree structure, a recursive conditional sampler, a coding construction with termination/limit proof, or a modularly weighted replacement for the shorted-operator disagreement bound. For 0<=B<=I and eta subset W, define S_eta(B)=B^(1/2)(I-P_closure(B^(1/2)l2(eta)))B^(1/2), b_B(x,eta)=<S_eta(B)delta_x,delta_x>. A proposed birth proof needs an averaged Lipschitz/control estimate for stationary coupled configurations and a same-iid limit. Derive weights and constants from the actual action; do not import an unweighted mass-transport identity. If you only solve Q_c=I/2+cA for a strict subinterval 0<=c<1/(2rho), state the exact range and remaining endpoint obligation. No silently imposed spectral gap.
