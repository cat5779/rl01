# G3: a DPP iid-factor theorem for unimodular Hecke actions

Frozen target: Gamma is any countable discrete group, H is a subgroup commensurated by every g in Gamma, and [H:H intersect gHg^(-1)]=[H:H intersect g^(-1)Hg]<infinity for every g. Let W=Gamma/H with the left action, and 0<=Q<=I a bounded self-adjoint Gamma-equivariant operator on l2(W). Prove or disprove that the DPP P^Q is a Gamma-equivariant measurable factor of iid Uniform[0,1] labels indexed by W. No amenability, spectral gap, free action, finite stabilizer or finite-range hypothesis may be added. The source is W-indexed, not Gamma-indexed. No finitary or Bernoulli-isomorphism claim is requested.

The structural starting point is a candidate birth construction whose free-action operator and Picard portion is supplied in SOURCE.md. In the free case its key step is an averaged trace estimate for JOINTLY stationary random covariant operators, not merely the deterministic commutant. Your main new bridge is to re-encode that step as the mass-transport identity on the balanced transitive action Gamma/H. Derive the orbit-counting formula rigorously and check whether E<B delta_H,delta_H> is a trace for the precise random operators used in shorting and polar decomposition. For invariant eta,zeta prove, refute or precisely restrict E|b_A(H,eta)-b_A(H,zeta)| <= P(eta(H) != zeta(H)). Do not infer this just because the deterministic root state is tracial.

If the estimate holds, carry it through the entire construction. Use t_s=1-exp(-s), A_s=exp(-s)Q(I-t_sQ)^(-1), sitewise iid PRMs and Picard iteration. Identify the law via terminal Z~P^Q thinned by independent exponential clocks: finite-window full-past hazards, moving-window shorting with a temporary Q>=cI, progressive common-filtration Poisson completion, then same-noise removal of c. A non-equivariant enumeration may be used in a proof of convergence but not to break stabilizer symmetry in the final factor. Supply the total Borel equivariant map and invariant exceptional-set convention. If only the transfer conditional on an unproved bridge is obtained, label it conditional and identify that bridge exactly.
