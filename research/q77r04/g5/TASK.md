# G5: turn the gapped DPP construction into a genuine finite-query certificate

Frozen target: Gamma is a countable discrete group, S a finite nonempty set, W=Gamma x S with the free left action, and Q is a bounded self-adjoint equivariant operator with 0<aI<=Q<=bI<I. Establish or refute the claim that P^Q is a finitary factor of iid Uniform[0,1] labels indexed by Gamma. Here finitary means a total equivariant Borel map Phi with an almost surely terminating procedure at each output coordinate: it queries finitely many whole site labels, produces a finite certificate, and that certificate fixes the output against every completion of the unqueried labels under the chosen total-map convention. Distinguish this from agreement only almost surely over a conditional distribution, and from an algorithm that merely happens to stabilize. One real site label may contain infinitely many bits; this is a finite-site claim, not finite-bit computability. The kernel is a fixed known mathematical parameter, not an oracle supplied by unqueried random labels.

If this precise finitary claim is valid, give the complete constructive proof and an explicit measurable finite certificate. Then derive whatever tail bound the proof actually yields in terms of the stated envelope/influence moduli; at minimum state a verifiable bound tending to zero if obtainable. Do not claim finite expected query count or a universal geometric-radius tail without proving it. If the all-completions convention is too strong, isolate an exact obstruction and state the closest proven definition without labelling it the frozen theorem. Do not infer a DPP non-factor result from the failure of this particular sampler.
