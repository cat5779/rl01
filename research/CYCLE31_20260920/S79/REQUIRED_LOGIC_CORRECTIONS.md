# Required logical qualifications from RL01 PR69

The hybrid m36,c=.8 counterexample disproves universal midpoint concavity and the paid-shell sign under layer bistochasticity alone. It does not refute every argument that does not explicitly use layer coherence, and it is not an exterior-unitary, consecutive-Fourier or true-sine counterexample.

For J=H_circle-H_U and K=H_hat-H_circle, the exact identity is H_U''=H_hat''-(K''+J''). Therefore actual finite concavity is equivalent to K''+J''>=H_hat'', not to K''+J''>=0. The latter is a stronger sufficient condition because H_hat''<0; requiring K'' and J'' separately nonnegative is stronger still. Nonnegative KL values do not imply nonnegative second derivatives.

The total-shell count-entropy input is the general Shepp–Olkin concavity theorem proved by Hillion and Johnson in2017, not a general theorem already proved in the1981 conjecture paper. Keep H_U, H_circle and H_hat distinct, and normalize by all2m sites.
