# Problem

For the genuine half-filled Fejer Toeplitz DPP at `c=19/20`, determine the
sign of

\[
\Gamma(c)=\int_0^1 u\,\mathbb E_{\infty,u}
\overline{\mathcal G}_{\infty,u}\,du,
\]

where `overline G` is the compensated local kernel (V14).  A positive answer
would imply `A_R''(0)>0` for all sufficiently large odd `R`; it still would
not by itself transfer the statement to the true entropy rate.

The strict computational subproblem is to evaluate the four residual pieces
under the physical-word law, not under spectral Bernoulli coordinates, and to
identify which sign closure remains viable at high contrast.
