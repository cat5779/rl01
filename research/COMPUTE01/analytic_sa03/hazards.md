# Hazards

1. Finite positive values do not determine `Gamma(19/20)`.
2. The local kernel is not positive word by word; any proof using that claim
   silently fails from `R=9` onward.
3. The signed transport term is genuinely negative and grows in magnitude.
4. High-`u` behavior is sharp enough that low-order quadrature is unreliable
   at `R=9`; convergence checks are required.
5. A positive `Gamma` proves eventual positivity only for the truncated
   production curvature.  The entropy-rate derivative bridge remains a
   separate obligation.
