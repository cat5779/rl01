# Hazards

- A positive lower bound on `A_k` alone does not imply `d_k>0`.
- The reference refill law is not a product law; its marginal pair structure
  must be included in every witness expectation.
- `theta_k` is the degree-two multiplier at the original clock, not a fitted
  heat parameter.
- Ordered and unordered pair sums differ by a factor of two.
- Pinsker constants depend on the stated total-variation convention.
- A fixed central layer does not determine the `w_l`-weighted window.
- Binary64 evidence cannot establish an eventual sign.
