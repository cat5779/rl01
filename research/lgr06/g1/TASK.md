# LG06 G1: low-cost connectivity repair assuming Q7.7

This is a NEW mathematical research task. The user's final goal is to explore paths toward AND attempt to solve the Lyons–Gaboriau cost/first L2-Betti conjecture, not to re-review Q7.7.

Primary target: for every infinite finitely generated countable group Gamma, prove cost(Gamma) = 1 + beta_1^(2)(Gamma), with cost(Gamma) explicitly defined as the infimum over essentially free pmp actions. If you obtain a statement about Bernoulli-action cost or every free pmp action/fixed price, distinguish it and prove the implication, never silently change the target. A genuine counterexample also counts. A complete new intermediate theorem must give its exact implication toward this target and the remaining obstruction; renamed equivalents and speculation are not a solution.

Context: on a Cayley graph, FUSF has expected root degree 2+2 beta_1^(2)(Gamma). The original Lyons–Gaboriau DPP route sought connected invariant finite-dependent DPP approximants dominating FUSF with arbitrarily small degree excess. ICM lab PR86 is a candidate bad family on F2 x F2, while PR87 and PR91 contain stronger candidate obstructions to every sufficiently close dominating Hermitian-DPP choice on specific product graphs. These are historical candidate results, not premises you must accept and not topics you should independently review in this task. Do not use the false shortcut 'FIID/finite dependence/closeness implies connected'. A repair need not remain a DPP.

Sources for precise definitions and known results: Russell Lyons ICM2014 survey, Theorem5.10 and surrounding paragraphs, https://rdlyons.pages.iu.edu/pdf/icm-pub.pdf ; Lyons–Thom Invariant Coupling of Determinantal Measures on Sofic Groups, https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.0969 ; Gaboriau cost/L2-Betti work and Abért–Weiss Bernoulli weak-containment/cost statements. Check exact hypotheses and directions from original sources when used. Group cost, cost of a particular action, fixed price, and treeability must not be conflated.

## Assigned mathematical route

Your route: directly attack low-cost measurable connectivity repair of FUSF or a carefully chosen FIID process. Try to prove, for every epsilon>0, existence of a Gamma-equivariant measurable additional edge process R with E deg_R(o)<=2 epsilon such that FUSF union R is connected a.s., using independent iid randomness and H77 where legitimate. R and the union need not be determinantal. If using larger generating sets, auxiliary graphings, or long edges, explicitly account for graphing cost and generation of the full orbit relation. Do not count a long path as one edge without a valid graphing convention. Explore multiscale sparse bridges, component geometry, mass transport, and measurable scheduling. Expose whether the proposed repair lemma is equivalent to or stronger than LG rather than a easier missing lemma. Your objective remains LG; prove the full cost implication when a construction works, or exhibit the precise obstruction and a genuinely weaker nontrivial lemma.
