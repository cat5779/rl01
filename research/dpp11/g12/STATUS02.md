# Second-result status

The unrestricted dimension-free selector theorem remains **INCOMPLETE**.

The second result proves several scoped statements: an exact affine representation of the entire flow polytope by one-point cover couplings at step (arepsilon/2); dimension-free fixed-gap atom regularity; singleton coordinate fibers and linear collapse near coordinate directions; exact support reduction; and Hausdorff stability with constants depending only on (arepsilon) and a fixed common support size, not on the ambient dimension.

Independent review narrows the interpretation. The result does not exclude all universal-selector obstructions. Full-support or unbounded-support pairwise separation, tail scales larger than the parameter distance, finite branching/cycle incompatibility, and global equivariant selection obstructions remain open. Uniform Hausdorff stability is one sufficient positive route, not a logically necessary one.
