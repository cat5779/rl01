PROVED; independent mathematical review: PASS.

For kernels block diagonal with respect to a supplied partition whose blocks have size at most a fixed (r), the explicit block selector handles arbitrary-support rank-one directions and has a dimension-free Lipschitz constant depending only on (\varepsilon,r). The theorem does not cover unrestricted non-diagonal kernels or comparisons without a common supplied partition. No novelty claim is made.
