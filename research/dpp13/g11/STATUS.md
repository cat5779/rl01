# Status

The restricted support-at-most-two selector theorem is proved. Two independent adversarial audits pass the conditioning, positivity, capacity, Lipschitz, support-transition, Borel, and permutation-covariance arguments.

The unrestricted selector theorem for arbitrary full-support rank-one directions remains open. This result does not claim that theorem, exact JO, an invariant weak process, or a strong/factor construction.
