# Current result: relative finitary DPP dynamics with a central cyclic direction

Start with [CENTER.md](CENTER.md). On any countable group with a central infinite-order element, it constructs explicit weak convolution backgrounds and spectral-zero increments with a whole-path relative finitary DPP process. Relative query count and first radius moment are finite in the constructed metric; composing the separate gapped initial sampler gives joint iid finitarity without transferring those moments. A nonamenable ambient group permits a noncommuting background.

The proof dependencies are [the positive-rate theorem](GENERAL.md), [local updates and corrected finite certificates](LOCAL.md), [the scalar Fourier kernel](FOURIER.md), and [the imported initial sampler](SAMPLER.md).

The cyclic, finitely generated product, and countable product derivations are earlier specializations of this one current family. Their frozen sources remain preserved in the research archive and source lineage. They are not additional current branches or separate solutions of general ordered coupling. The integer Heisenberg group times F2 is covered and cannot split off a direct Z factor.

The central infinite-order element, weighted-small background, constructed increment and constructed metric are explicit restrictions. No claim covers arbitrary groups, increments, backgrounds, or a preassigned-metric moment bound. Classical graphical and Fourier tools are not claimed new. See [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md) and [SOURCE.json](SOURCE.json) for scoped review and source/output identities. This remains a research draft without external referee acceptance, complete probabilistic formalization or novelty certification.
