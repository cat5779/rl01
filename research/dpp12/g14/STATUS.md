# Status

The abstract ordinary countable-coordinate pure-birth realization theorem is **PROVED** and has passed an independent scoped mathematical review.

It yields a prescribed-marginal, coordinatewise càdlàg weak martingale solution with at most one jump per coordinate and no simultaneous positive-time jumps. It does not assert an invariant path law, a strong or Poisson realization, pathwise uniqueness, or a factor-of-iid construction. In the DPP application, existence of a suitable covariant rate field satisfying the abstract hypotheses remains a separate input.
