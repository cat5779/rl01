# From invariant ordered DPP couplings to joint iid realizations

Fix a countable discrete group Gamma acting regularly on itself. Let 0<=A<=B<=I be complex Hermitian operators commuting with left translation. The target is: for EVERY fixed ordered pair (A,B), there exists a total Borel Gamma-equivariant map F:[0,1]^Gamma -> {0,1}^Gamma x {0,1}^Gamma whose marginals are mu_A and mu_B and whose outputs X,Y satisfy X subset Y almost surely. If all-input containment is claimed, give the invariant exceptional-input convention. The map may depend on the pair: simultaneous Borel dependence on all kernels or a grand coupling of all kernels is NOT part of the frozen target.

This is a construction question about the joint law, not separate marginal samplers. You may explicitly assume H: every fixed equivariant positive contraction has an equivariant iid DPP factor. You may also explicitly assume L: every fixed ordered pair has an invariant monotone law-level coupling. Label a result conditional on H and/or L. Neither assumption gives an equivariant conditional sampler, finitary code, measurable family of couplings, or closure of factors under weak limits.

Find a rigorous upgrade, or a precise obstruction to a proposed upgrade. Prefer a genuinely different mechanism from the FK scalar-path dynamics: possible routes include an order-preserving marked/coloured DPP representation on a finite free extension Gamma x S, dilation/flags/exterior algebra, an explicit equivariant randomization theorem with proved hypotheses, or a quantitative relative-factor approximation theorem. A failure of one representation is not a counterexample to the main target. Do not replace an unknown step by a theorem equivalent to the target.

A construction input worth testing is a relative transition for spectrally gapped finite-support positive increments: given X with law mu_C, independent clocks, and C_t=C+tTT*, a birth process adds points and has marginals mu_{C_t}. A complete candidate uses finite-block flow selection, invariant mean sensitivity, finite-range envelopes and marginal identification. This relative transition is a hypothesis R unless you independently derive it. If using approximations, prove the actual convergence of joint iid maps on common input, rather than taking a weak limit of factor laws. Explain whether a summable disagreement estimate suffices and how the ordered constraint is preserved throughout.

Maintain the distinction between finite free labels and arbitrary actions with stabilizers. The target source is regular Gamma-iid. Do not silently enlarge the source, assume amenability/soficity, impose a hidden spectral gap, or use an arbitrary enumeration that destroys equivariance. Address complex kernels and singular endpoints. Test trivial/finite groups, abelian groups, free groups, scalar and projection kernels. Prove every marginal by finite inclusion probabilities or another complete identification argument.

A positive theorem should yield a precise reusable interface in a small theory. For comparison, law-level ordered couplings imply the sharp inequality dbar(mu_A,mu_B)<=tau|A-B| for general unordered kernels by a scaled polygonal path; they do not automatically yield the joint iid conclusion. FK(Q)I need not be <=Q, so even the requested theorem does not by itself solve the FK coupling problem. State exact implications and remaining gaps.

Use the original authors' strongest reasonable mathematical intention, while imposing strict proof standards on our own claims. Report known tools with primary references, new proved lemmas, open/equivalent blockers, and whether any claimed new theorem is already contained in prior work. Primary sources: Lyons–Thom, https://rdlyons.pages.iu.edu/pdf/couple-pub.pdf (Theorem5.1 and Question7.7); Lyons, https://rdlyons.pages.iu.edu/pdf/icm-pub.pdf (explicit monotone couplings and section5.2).
