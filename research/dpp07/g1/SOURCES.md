# Prior methods and the scope of the construction

## Conditional intensities and repulsion

Georgii and Yoo, *Conditional Intensity and Gibbsianness of Determinantal Point Processes* (2004), study Papangelou intensities, their repulsive monotonicity, and conditions for Gibbs descriptions. Conditional-intensity monotonicity is an established ingredient; it is not a novelty claim of the present argument. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401402).

## Earlier discrete determinantal dynamics

Chae and Yoo, *Glauber and Kawasaki Dynamics for Determinantal Point Processes in Discrete Spaces* (2009), construct equilibrium Feller dynamics. Their Theorem 4.2 imposes diagonal dominance on the odds operator. Equations (3.1)–(3.2) use a sum of configuration-wise supremum influences; Section 4 establishes that condition under the added operator assumption. The paper also points to earlier discrete dynamics by Shirai and Yoo. [Published paper](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/iis/15/3/15_3_377/_pdf).

The construction considered here instead follows a specified, nonstationary FK path. Its sensitivity estimate is averaged over jointly invariant pairs, and its proposed initial source is independent Bernoulli variables and independent Poisson measures. The cited equilibrium theorem, under its stated hypotheses, does not directly furnish this path or this joint endpoint factor.

## Remaining attribution question

These distinctions concern the exact statements checked above. They do not establish priority for the FK path, the averaged transport argument, the envelope construction, or their combination. An exhaustive novelty assessment remains open. No general claim that determinantal dynamics or conditional-odds constructions are new is warranted.
