# An equivariant coupling along the Fuglede–Kadison path

Let Gamma be a countable discrete group acting on itself by left translation. Let Q be a complex Hermitian positive contraction in the commutant, tau the normalized group trace, p=exp(tau(log Q)), and mu_Q its determinantal law.

The target is a single joint Gamma-equivariant iid construction (X,Y) with X distributed as Bernoulli(p)^Gamma, Y distributed as mu_Q, and X contained in Y almost surely. First solve the genuinely infinite, two-sided spectrally gapped case epsilon I <= Q <= (1-epsilon)I; distinguish this from a full endpoint theorem. A complete obstruction to a specified construction is useful but does not disprove the target.

The proposed bridge is a FK-level-set path, not Loewner order. For t>=0 set a_t=p/FK(Q+tI), K_t=a_t(Q+tI), r_t=a_t tau(K_t^{-1}); then K'_t=a_t I-r_t K_t and K_t tends to pI. For a finite DPP let q_i(eta) be the conditional occupation probability at i given the entire other configuration eta; q_i is a probability, whereas q_i/(1-q_i) is its Papangelou odds. The death rate is r_t-a_t/q_i on occupied sites. Its reversed birth rate is (r_t q_i-a_t)/(1-q_i), with the time orientation and time change derived explicitly. A finite forward-equation identity is supplied in the accompanying note.

Core task: derive a rigorous infinite-volume FK-path coupling from Bernoulli labels and independent iid clocks, or locate a precise failure of this programme. Develop a reusable criterion if justified: finite-block conditional flows plus an invariant mean sensitivity estimate, finite-range envelopes and coalescence, followed by exact marginal identification. A worst-configuration influence sum is not available. No averaging over a nonamenable group. Starting at the limit t=infinity is a real entrance-law issue; a finite-time construction from mu_{K_T} plus a weak limit of joint laws proves neither a joint iid factor nor a canonical limit.

For orientation, an ordered-kernel candidate uses local positive increments, finite-block Lipschitz flow selection, random-covariant tracial mean sensitivity E osc_[U,V] c_e <= C P(U_e!=V_e), and nested finite-range envelopes p_R(t)<=2 delta_R t exp(Ct). This is a possible mechanism, not a theorem granted for the FK direction. Derive its FK hypotheses and time-integrability, including near the scalar entrance. Conditional probabilities on null exterior configurations need a canonical measurable version and a proved agreement with the DPP conditional law.

You may assume the marginal sampling hypothesis H: each fixed equivariant Hermitian positive contraction has a total Borel equivariant iid factor. H does not grant a monotone joint factor or an equivariant disintegration of an arbitrary coupling. Label every use of H.

State all quantifiers: a construction for each fixed kernel versus simultaneous measurable kernel dependence; regular-Gamma input versus other index sets; a.s. versus all-input equivariance; endpoint law versus path law; exact uniqueness class. Do not silently request or claim a grand coupling over all kernels. Treat the original authors' intended question substantively and our proposed proof skeptically. Do not substitute a weak statement for the target.

Deliver a complete proof, counterexample, or precisely delimited incomplete result. Tag imported lemmas with exact primary-source hypotheses; separate classical facts from new deductions. A small theory must have an explicit reusable theorem and verified applications, not just a common vocabulary.
