# Formulation lineage

- **28 February 2006:** Richard Evan Schwartz's expository manuscript “Billiards Obtuse and Irrational” defined the triangle parameter space \(\Delta\), stable words, and orbit tiles \(O(W)\). It stated **Conjecture 4.1: “An orbit tile is connected and simply connected.”** Author PDF: [Brown University](https://www.math.brown.edu/~res/Papers/intel.pdf).
- **2009:** “Obtuse Triangular Billiards II” reported rigorous work with many orbit tiles and repeated that the general topology assertion had no proof.
- **ICM 2022 / publication 2023:** Schwartz restated the same assertion, with the same objects and quantifiers, as the formally numbered **Conjecture 5.7** in his ICM survey.

The 2006 Conjecture 4.1 is the exact pre-ICM formulation, not merely a related precursor. The ICM statement is a renumbered restatement in the later survey.
