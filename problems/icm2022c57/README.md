# ICM 2022 Conjecture 5.7: topology of triangular orbit tiles

This directory freezes **Conjecture 5.7** from Richard Evan Schwartz's invited article in the *Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2022*.

For every stable periodic bounce word in a labeled Euclidean triangle, the conjecture says that the complete parameter region supporting that word is connected and simply connected.

Audit classification on 2026-09-28: **VERIFIED_OPEN**. The ICM article presents the assertion as a conjecture based on numerical experiments; a forward-source, author-page, and exact-phrase audit found no later proof or counterexample. The adjacent Schwartz Conjecture 5.10 material in the ICM result repository is a different problem and does not process Conjecture 5.7.

Files:

- `problem.md`: frozen statement and topology conventions.
- `source.md`: numbered ICM source and surrounding definitions.
- `status.md`: current-status and processed-work audit.
- `lineage.md`: the 2006 pre-ICM formulation and its relation to the ICM statement.

This package intentionally contains no suggested attack route or mathematical hint.
