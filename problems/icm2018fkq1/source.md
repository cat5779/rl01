# Primary source

Bassam Fayad and Raphaël Krikorian, “Some Questions Around Quasi-Periodic Dynamics,” in *Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2018*, Vol. III, pp. 1927–1950.

- Author-hosted article PDF: <https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~bassam.fayad/ICM_FayadKrikorian-cor.pdf>
- Proceedings location: Section 2, printed p. 1928.
- Formal label: **Question 1**.
- Exact printed question: a smooth diffeomorphism of \(\mathbb T^d\) is topologically conjugate to a Diophantine translation; “Is the conjugacy smooth?”

The article defines Diophantine translation vectors immediately before Section 2 and places the question in \(\mathrm{Diff}_0^\infty(\mathbb T^d)\). The frozen statement restores that context and removes an erroneous older normalized summary about analytic pseudo-rotations; pseudo-rotations are not part of Question 1.
