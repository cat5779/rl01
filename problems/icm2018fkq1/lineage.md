# Lineage and adjacent results

- In dimension one, Denjoy theory supplies topological conjugacy under the standard regularity hypotheses, and the Herman–Yoccoz theorem upgrades the conjugacy to smoothness for a Diophantine rotation number.
- Standard KAM theory gives a positive higher-dimensional answer when \(f\) lies in a sufficiently small neighborhood of \(T_\alpha\), with the neighborhood depending on \(\alpha\).
- Fayad–Krikorian's ICM 2018 Question 1 asks for a **global** conclusion from topological conjugacy alone.
- Ghazouani–Ulcigrai (2023) calls the higher-dimensional assertion Krikorian's rigidity conjecture while proving an analogous result for a different class of surface foliations.

No identical formally numbered pre-ICM statement was located after searches of the cited rigidity literature and the authors' earlier papers. The ICM article is therefore the earliest exact formal statement located in this audit; no stronger priority claim is made.

The remaining obligation is precisely the global \(d\ge2\) assertion in `problem.md`.
