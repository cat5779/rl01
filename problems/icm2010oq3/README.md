# ICM 2010 Open Question 3: self-normalized saddlepoint expansion

This directory freezes **Open Question 3** from Qi-Man Shao's invited article in the *Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2010*.

The question asks whether the displayed Lugannani--Rice-type expansion for a self-normalized sum remains valid after the Fourier-integrability hypothesis used in the preceding theorem is removed, first for fixed \(x\) and then uniformly for a shrinking lower endpoint.

Audit classification on 2026-09-28: **VERIFIED_OPEN**. The exact two-part question is not covered by the later strongly-nonlattice theorem or by the 2025 first-order exact large-deviation results. No matching proof, disproof, partial attempt, continuation, or review draft was found in the audited ICM result repository or in the public collaboration repository.

Files:

- `problem.md`: frozen mathematical statement and boundary conventions.
- `source.md`: ICM numbering and source context.
- `status.md`: current-status and processed-work audit.
- `lineage.md`: pre-ICM formulation lineage.

This is a problem package only. It intentionally contains no proposed proof strategy or hint.
