# ICM source and numbering

Primary source:

Qi-Man Shao, “Stein's Method, Self-normalized Limit Theory and Applications,” *Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2010, Hyderabad*, Vol. IV, pp. 2325–2350, DOI [10.1142/9789814324359_0148](https://doi.org/10.1142/9789814324359_0148). Official proceedings PDF: [IMU](https://www.mathunion.org/fileadmin/ICM/Proceedings/ICM2010.4/ICM2010.4.pdf).

The item is formally labeled **Open Question 3** on p. 2340. It immediately follows Theorem 3.1 and equation (3.4). Theorem 3.1 assumes
\[
\int_{\mathbb R^2}\left|\mathbb E e^{isX+itX^2}\right|^r\,ds\,dt<\infty
\quad\text{for some }r>1
\tag{3.3}
\]
and obtains the expansion with an \(O(n^{-1})\) term inside the parentheses. Open Question 3 asks whether, without (3.3), equation (3.5) holds with \(O(n^{-1/2})\), first for fixed \(x\), and then uniformly on \([a_n,1/2]\) when \(a_n\downarrow0\) and \(a_n\sqrt n\to\infty\).

The formulas for \(K\), the saddlepoint pair, \(\Delta\), \(\Lambda_0\), \(\Lambda_1\), \(w\), and \(v\) are taken from the immediately preceding source text. The admissibility clause in `problem.md` makes explicit the regularity already required for those source formulas to be meaningful; it does not add the forbidden condition (3.3).
