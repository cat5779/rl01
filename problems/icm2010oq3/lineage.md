# Formulation lineage

- **2004:** Jing, Shao, and Zhou published the saddlepoint expansion under the joint characteristic-function integrability condition. This supplies the formula and the conditional theorem from which the later open question arose.
- **2006:** Zhou and Jing removed that integrability condition in the strongly nonlattice case and explicitly left lattice extensions open.
- **2010:** Shao's ICM article assembled the exact definitions and formally posed the two-part problem as **Open Question 3**.

No pre-ICM source was located that states the full two-part question with both the fixed-\(x\) \(O(n^{-1/2})\) correction and the uniform range \(a_n\le x\le1/2\) as a numbered open problem. The 2004 and 2006 papers are therefore recorded as the precise technical lineage, not as identical earlier formulations.
