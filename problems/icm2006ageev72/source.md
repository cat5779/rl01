# Primary source

Oleg N. Ageev, “On spectral invariants in modern ergodic theory,” in *Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians, Madrid 2006*, Vol. II, European Mathematical Society, 2006, pp. 1641–1653.

- Official proceedings PDF: <https://www.mathunion.org/fileadmin/ICM/Proceedings/ICM2006.2/ICM2006.2.ocr.pdf#page=1651>
- Location: Section 7, printed p. 1651.
- Formal label: **7.2**.
- Printed wording: “(A well-known problem.) Is it true that every rank one transformation has a singular spectrum?”

The definitions in `problem.md` make explicit the standard probability-preserving rank-one and spectral conventions used by the article. They do not broaden the source to rank-one flows, nonsingular maps, or infinite-measure systems.
