# Lineage and adjacent results

The ICM article calls the item “a well-known problem”; it is not presented as a new 2006 conjecture.

- Bourgain (1993) proved almost-sure singular maximal spectral type for Ornstein's random class-one/rank-one construction.
- Klemes–Reinhold (1997) proved singularity for rank-one transformations satisfying the sufficient cutting-growth condition \(\sum_n r_n^{-2}=\infty\), where \(r_n\) is the number of cuts at stage \(n\).
- Ageev's ICM 2006 Problem 7.2 records the unrestricted question with no growth, randomness, mixing, or bounded-cut hypothesis.
- Aistleitner–Hofer (2012) obtained further restricted-class results but did not settle the unrestricted problem.

The earliest precise pre-ICM source located for the general singular-spectrum program is Klemes–Reinhold (1997); the problem itself was already regarded as well known. No claim of first formulation is made here.

The remaining obligation is exactly the unrestricted universal statement in `problem.md`.
