# Primary source and fixed definition

Bruce Kleiner, “Developments in 3D Ricci flow since Perelman,” *Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2022*, Vol. 4, pp. 2376–2390.

- DOI and open chapter: <https://doi.org/10.4171/ICM2022/150>
- EMS PDF: <https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/33206>
- Location: printed p. 2386.
- Formal label: **Conjecture 3.14**.

Immediately before the conjecture, the article states that the set of noncompact times has Minkowski dimension at most \(1/2\), then asks for the stronger finite-set and componentwise structural assertions frozen here.

The phrase “singular Ricci flow” is fixed to the standard object developed in:

- Bruce Kleiner and John Lott, “Singular Ricci flows I,” *Acta Math.* 219 (2017), 65–134, arXiv <https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.2271>, especially the definitions of Ricci-flow spacetime and singular Ricci flow.

That referenced definition, including its completeness and canonical-neighborhood requirements, is incorporated by reference. It must not be replaced by an arbitrary weak Ricci flow or by a Ricci flow with an independently chosen surgery schedule.
