# Lineage and adjacent results

- Kleiner–Lott introduced singular Ricci flows and proved existence as limits of Ricci flows with surgery; the preprint first appeared in 2014 and the journal version in 2017.
- Bamler–Kleiner later proved uniqueness and stability of Ricci flow through singularities, making the object canonical for compact initial data.
- Before Conjecture 3.14, the ICM article lists structural results for the time slices and the theorem that noncompact times have Minkowski dimension at most \(1/2\).
- The ICM conjecture strengthens this to finiteness and adds the componentwise extinction/finite-neckpinch alternative.

No pre-ICM paper containing the exact two-clause statement was located. The earliest exact formal formulation found is the ICM 2022 article itself. This is a lineage report, not a priority claim.

The remaining obligation is the complete two-part statement in `problem.md`; proving only the first clause or only a restricted version of the second is partial progress.
