# ICM 2010 Open Question 5: Studentized U-statistics

This directory freezes **Open Question 5** from Qi-Man Shao's invited article in the *Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2010*.

The question asks whether the two moderate-deviation conclusions of the immediately preceding theorem remain true for every nondegenerate Studentized U-statistic after the theorem's pointwise domination condition on the kernel is removed.

Audit classification on 2026-09-28: **VERIFIED_OPEN**. Later work retains additional kernel control or proves different approximations; no complete proof or valid counterexample to the unrestricted ICM statement was located. No matching material was found in the audited ICM result repository, the prior P1–P6 dispatch, or the current public collaboration repository.

Files:

- `problem.md`: frozen statement and conventions.
- `source.md`: ICM numbering and source context.
- `status.md`: current-status and processed-work audit.
- `lineage.md`: pre-ICM submission and formulation lineage.

This package intentionally gives no proposed proof route or hint.
