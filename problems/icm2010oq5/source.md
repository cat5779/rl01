# ICM source and numbering

Primary source:

Qi-Man Shao, “Stein's Method, Self-normalized Limit Theory and Applications,” *Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2010, Hyderabad*, Vol. IV, pp. 2325–2350, DOI [10.1142/9789814324359_0148](https://doi.org/10.1142/9789814324359_0148). Official proceedings PDF: [IMU](https://www.mathunion.org/fileadmin/ICM/Proceedings/ICM2010.4/ICM2010.4.pdf).

Section 3.4 defines \(U_n\), \(T_n\), \(R_n\), and \(q_i\). On p. 2345, Theorem 3.5 gives the two conclusions in `problem.md` under condition (3.15), and the next displayed item is formally labeled:

> **Open Question 5.** Does Theorem 3.5 hold without assuming condition (3.15)?

The centered form used in `problem.md` is exactly the translation-invariant rewriting obtained by replacing \(h\) with \(h-\theta\). This leaves \(U_n-\theta\), \(q_i-U_n\), \(R_n\), and \(T_n\) unchanged and writes the source condition without its implicit centering convention.
