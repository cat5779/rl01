# Formulation lineage

- **20 September 2008:** Lai, Shao, and Wang's manuscript “Cramér type moderate deviations for Studentized U-statistics” was received by *ESAIM: Probability and Statistics*.
- **2 June 2009:** the manuscript was revised. It proves the two target asymptotics under the pointwise kernel condition later numbered (3.15) in the ICM article. The journal version appeared in 2011 under DOI [10.1051/ps/2009014](https://doi.org/10.1051/ps/2009014).
- **2010:** Shao's ICM article restated that theorem as Theorem 3.5 and formally asked, as **Open Question 5**, whether its condition can be removed.

No earlier source was located that states the unrestricted assertion itself as a numbered conjecture or open problem. The 2008/2009 manuscript is the exact pre-ICM theorem/submission lineage from which the ICM question was formed.
