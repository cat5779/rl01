\title{From DPP Rate Equations to Finitary Birth Dynamics\\
\large Weak laws, common-noise realizations, and finite certificates on countable groups}
\author{Anonymous working draft}
\date{October 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Fix a time-indexed determinantal process and a covariant pure-birth rate field
that satisfies its finite-cylinder continuity equations.  Several inequivalent
realization questions arise: an ordinary weak law, an invariant weak law, a
path determined by the supplied initial state and fresh Poisson noise, and a
path whose individual coordinates admit finite certificates.  We keep these
notions separate and give sufficient conditions for the stronger levels.

Our main construction is a finite-rate-box criterion.  It controls the
worst-case oscillation of the rate over finite lower--upper boxes by an Osgood
modulus and yields a total equivariant relative realization with an almost
sure finite arbitrary-completion certificate for every coordinate path.  A
summable local-update hypothesis implies the rate-box criterion.  For
time-dependent mixture weights, this implication requires an explicit
integrable factorization of the mixture tails.  The direct local-update
construction additionally gives first moments for the number and radius of
relative queries.

Examples separate the criteria.  A genuinely nonlocal diagonal-DPP rate field
satisfies the rate-box condition but has no finite-mean local-neighborhood
mixture.  A static two-orbit example has a causal strong realization but no
relative finite certificate.  Finally, groups containing an infinite-order
element admit a non-diagonal DPP family with a relative finitary realization
and finite first query and radius moments in a constructed proper metric.
These are sufficient theorems, not a solution of invariant weak existence for
arbitrary Borel prescribed rates on nonamenable groups.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

A finite-cylinder forward equation specifies how the desired marginals should
move, but it does not specify how much randomness or locality is needed to
realize that movement.  This distinction is particularly sharp for
determinantal point processes (DPPs).  The same curve of DPP marginals may be
realized only as a weak martingale law, may admit a common-noise construction,
or may admit a finitary algorithm whose answer at one coordinate is certified
after finitely many input labels have been exposed.

This paper organizes these conclusions into a hierarchy.  Let a countable
group $\Gamma$ act on a countable coordinate set $I$, let
$\mu_t$ be an invariant curve on $\{0,1\}^I$, and let
\[
 a_i(t,x)=(1-x_i)c_i(t,x)
\]
be covariant pure-birth rates.  The finite-cylinder continuity equation is
\begin{equation}
 \mu_t(f)-\mu_0(f)
 =\int_0^t\mu_s\!\left(\sum_i a_i(s,x)
 [f(x^{i,1})-f(x)]\right)\,ds
 \label{eq:ce}
\end{equation}
for every cylinder function $f$.  Equation~\eqref{eq:ce} is necessary for a
prescribed-marginal dynamics, but it does not imply invariance, pathwise
uniqueness, common-noise measurability, or finite certificates.

The main result starts from an ordinary weak realization and adds a geometric
condition on the rate field.  Expose only finitely many coordinates, allow all
outside completions, and compute the exact lower and upper possible rates.
If the expected gap of these two thresholds closes with an Osgood modulus, the
lower and upper graphical envelope processes coalesce.  Their eventual
agreement produces a finite certificate for the complete coordinate path.

The second contribution compares this criterion with a more concrete
summable local-update condition.  The comparison is not merely formal when
the mixture weights depend on time: a tail that is small pointwise in time is
not automatically bounded by a fixed deterministic sequence.  We supply the
required $L^1$ tail factorization and obtain a linear Osgood modulus.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The results do not prove arbitrary-group invariant weak existence, a general
strong factor construction for unbounded or infinite-range rates, or exact
ordered joint iid sampling for arbitrary DPP kernels.  These remain distinct
open problems.

\section{Five realization levels}

The following table records the outputs used throughout the paper.

                >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.34\textwidth}}
\toprule
Level & Output & Not included automatically\\
\midrule
Ordinary weak & A martingale law with marginals $\mu_t$ and rates $a$ & Invariance, common noise, uniqueness\\
Invariant weak & An invariant path law with the same properties & An iid or Poisson construction\\
Relative strong & An equivariant measurable path from supplied $X_0$ and fresh product noise & Finite certificates\\
Relative finitary & Each coordinate path is fixed by finitely many complete input labels under every outside completion & Query moments\\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

Weak convergence of joint laws does not preserve a common-input factor
representation.  Likewise, separate iid samplers for two marginals do not
produce an ordered joint factor.  Each arrow in the hierarchy therefore needs
its own argument.

\section{Common setup and weak inputs}

For clarity, first take $I=\Gamma$ with the regular action.  Write $x^{g,b}$
for the configuration obtained by setting coordinate $g$ to $b$.  Intrinsic
rates $c_g$ are independent of the center bit and actual rates are
\[
 a_g(t,x)=(1-x_g)c_g(t,x).
\]
Every pure-birth rate that vanishes at occupied sites has this form after
setting $c_g(t,x)=a_g(t,x^{g,0})$.

The ordinary weak-existence input assumes a weakly continuous prescribed
curve, finite-valued jointly Borel rates, equation~\eqref{eq:ce}, and
\[
 \int_0^1\mu_t(a_i(t,\cdot))\,dt<\infty
\]
for every coordinate.  It yields a coordinatewise cadlag pure-birth
martingale law with the prescribed marginals and no simultaneous
positive-time births.  In the linear DPP application one takes
$K_t=C+tH$, with $H\succeq0$ and
\[
 \eps I\preceq K_t\preceq(1-\eps)I.
\]

For bounded rates, a weak law in this pure-birth, no-simultaneous-birth class
admits a compatible enlargement by independent sitewise Poisson random
measures.  Compatibility means that future product-noise increments are
independent of the common past and that the path accepts exactly the points
below its current rate.

For countable amenable groups, averaging the compact convex solution set gives
an invariant prescribed-marginal weak law.  In the stated linear DPP setting,
for arbitrary countable groups, invariant weak existence is equivalent to a
local endpoint condition along a vanishing-mesh sequence of partitions:
every adjacent time interval must admit a diagonally invariant dynamically
realizable endpoint law.  Existence of these local invariant endpoint
elements remains open in general.

\section{Finite rate boxes}

Let $F_1\subset F_2\subset\cdots$ be finite sets containing the identity.
Given lower and upper patterns $l\le u$ on $gF_R$, let
\[
 B_g^R(l,u)=\{z:l_j\le z_j\le u_j\text{ for }j\in gF_R\}.
\]
Define exact intrinsic extrema
\begin{equation}
 \ell_g^R(t,l,u)=\inf_{z\in B_g^R(l,u)}c_g(t,z),\qquad
 h_g^R(t,l,u)=\sup_{z\in B_g^R(l,u)}c_g(t,z).
 \label{eq:extrema}
\end{equation}

\begin{definition}[BOX]
Both extrema in~\eqref{eq:extrema} are jointly Borel in time and the finite
patterns.
\end{definition}

Drive lower and upper finite-range envelope processes by the same proposal
field using $\ell^R$ and $h^R$.  Let
\[
 \delta_R(t)=\Pp(L_e^R(t)\ne U_e^R(t))
\]
and let $W_e^R(t)$ be the current threshold gap.

\begin{definition}[WIDTH]
There exist constants $A_n,\tau_n\ge0$, with $\tau_n\to0$, and
$L\in L^1[0,1]$ such that, outside one null time set,
\begin{equation}
 \E W_e^R(t)\le L(t)(A_n\delta_R(t)+\tau_n),\qquad R\ge n.
 \label{eq:width}
\end{equation}
Put
\[
 \omega(r)=\inf_n(A_nr+\tau_n).
\]
We impose either $\omega\equiv0$, or $\omega(r)>0$ for $r>0$ and
\begin{equation}
 \int_{0+}\frac{dr}{\omega(r)}=\infty.
 \label{eq:osgood}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Shrinking rate boxes]
Assume ordinary weak existence, a uniform finite rate bound $M$, compatible
Poisson completion, BOX, WIDTH, and~\eqref{eq:osgood}.  Then there is a total
Borel exactly equivariant relative realization of the prescribed process.
Almost surely, every complete coordinate path has a finite
arbitrary-completion certificate.  The prescribed-marginal weak law is unique
in the stated class and invariant.  If the initial law has a finitary iid
sampler with the same certificate convention, then the full path is an iid
finitary process after composition.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof roadmap]
At fixed $R$, the backward ancestor graph is finite because strict-time chains
of length $k$ have expected count at most $(M|F_R|)^k/k!$.  Exact extrema make
the envelope systems nested in $R$, while compatible Poisson completion traps
every weak reference path between them.  Compensation gives
\[
 \delta_R(t)\le\int_0^t\E W_e^R(s)\,ds.
\]
After taking $R\to\infty$ in the correct order,
\eqref{eq:width}--\eqref{eq:osgood} and Osgood's lemma force the limiting
discrepancy to vanish.  Each coordinate chooses its birth time from a fixed
finite list, so agreement at all times upgrades to eventual equality of the
entire coordinate paths at some finite radius.  The least successful local
computation supplies the arbitrary-completion certificate.
\end{proof}

The theorem asserts no query-size or radius moment, no simultaneous
all-initial collapse event, and no result for arbitrary unbounded rates.

\section{Summable local updates imply shrinking boxes}

\begin{definition}[LOCAL]
Suppose the actual rates have the decomposition
\begin{equation}
 a_g(t,x)=\sum_{k\ge0}\lambda_k(t)
 b_k\bigl(t,(x_{gv})_{v\in V_k}\bigr),
 \label{eq:local}
\end{equation}
where $V_k$ is finite and contains the identity,
$\lambda_k:[0,1]\to[0,\infty)$ is Borel,
$b_k:[0,1]\times\{0,1\}^{V_k}\to[0,1]$ is Borel and vanishes when the
center is occupied, and
\begin{equation}
 \sum_k\lambda_k(t)|V_k|\le B<\infty
 \quad\text{for every }t.
 \label{eq:budget}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LOCAL implies BOX+WIDTH]
Under~\eqref{eq:local}--\eqref{eq:budget}, the center-independent intrinsic
rate satisfies BOX and WIDTH for a nested sequence of finite sets.  One may
take $A_n=B$, $\tau_n=2^{-n}$, and an integrable $L\ge1$.  Consequently
$\omega(r)=Br$, with the zero case understood separately.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Replace each component by its center-vacant version.  Then
\[
 c_e(t,x)=\sum_k\lambda_k(t)\widetilde b_k(t,x|_{V_k}).
\]
For fixed time the series converges uniformly in $x$ because
$\sum_k\lambda_k(t)\le B$.  Thus $c_e(t,\cdot)$ is continuous.  On every
finite cylinder, exact extrema may be computed over a countable dense family
of extensions, so they are Borel in time.  Hence BOX holds.

Put
\[
 s_N(t)=\sum_{k>N}\lambda_k(t).
\]
Then $0\le s_N\le B$ and $s_N(t)\downarrow0$.  Dominated convergence permits
a strictly increasing sequence $N_n$ such that
\[
 \int_0^1s_{N_n}(t)\,dt\le2^{-2n}.
\]
Set
\begin{equation}
 L(t)=1+\sum_{n\ge1}2^ns_{N_n}(t).
 \label{eq:Lfactor}
\end{equation}
Then $L\in L^1$, $L\ge1$, and
$s_{N_n}(t)\le2^{-n}L(t)$ outside one null set.  Let
\[
 F_n=\bigcup_{k\le N_n}V_k.
\]
These sets are finite, nested, and contain the identity.

For $R\ge n$, a component $k\le N_n$ contributes no width if the two
left-limit envelope configurations agree on $V_k$, and otherwise contributes
at most one.  The tail contributes at most $s_{N_n}(t)$.  Translation
invariance and a union bound give
\begin{align*}
 \E W_e^R(t)
 &\le\sum_{k\le N_n}\lambda_k(t)|V_k|\delta_R(t)+s_{N_n}(t)\\
 &\le B\delta_R(t)+2^{-n}L(t)\\
 &\le L(t)(B\delta_R(t)+2^{-n}).
\end{align*}
This is WIDTH.  Finally
\[
 \omega(r)=\inf_n(Br+2^{-n})=Br,
\]
which satisfies the Osgood condition when $B>0$; if $B=0$, all rates vanish.
\end{proof}

The factorization~\eqref{eq:Lfactor} is essential at this level of
generality.  The simpler choice
$\tau_n=\sum_{k>n}\lambda_k$ is valid for time-independent weights but is not
a deterministic WIDTH constant for arbitrary $\lambda_k(t)$.

\subsection{Quantitative addendum}

The direct LOCAL graphical construction gives more than the qualitative
conclusion of the rate-box theorem.  If the weights are constant and
\[
 B_1=\sum_k\lambda_k\sum_{v\in V_k}|v|<\infty
\]
in a fixed left-invariant metric, then the expected number of relative queried
labels is at most $e^B$, and the expected maximum query radius is at most
$B_1e^B$.  These moments need not survive composition with a separate initial
iid sampler.

\section{Mean Osgood uniqueness}

Rate boxes control every outside completion.  A weaker, nonfinitary conclusion
follows from a condition involving only couplings of the prescribed marginals.
Assume the covariant rates are bounded, an ordinary prescribed-marginal weak
law exists in the no-simultaneous-birth class, $L\in L^1[0,1]$, and
$\omega$ is continuous, nondecreasing, vanishes at zero, is positive away
from zero, and satisfies~\eqref{eq:osgood}.  Suppose that, outside one fixed
null set of times, every coupling $(U,V)$ with both laws equal to $\mu_t$
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \sup_i\E|a_i(t,U)-a_i(t,V)|
 \le L(t)\omega\!\left(\sup_j\Pp(U_j\ne V_j)\right).
 \label{eq:mean}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Mean Osgood extraction]
Under the preceding assumptions, compatible conditional copies driven by the
same Poisson field agree.  The conditional law given the initial state and
noise is almost surely Dirac.  Hence the prescribed weak law is unique within
the prescribed-$\mu_t$ martingale class, invariant, and has a causal relative
strong realization.
\end{theorem}

This theorem gives no finite certificate and is not an all-initial-law
uniqueness theorem.

\section{Examples and separations}

\subsection{A nonlocal rate-box example}

For every countably infinite group $G$, take $\Gamma=G\times C_2$, pair each
site with its $C_2$ partner, and use a lexicographic comparison of two
enumerated rays to perturb opposite rates.  The perturbations cancel in every
cylinder equation, so the prescribed marginals remain iid Bernoulli and hence
diagonal DPPs.  Finite prefixes give
\[
 \E W_e^R(t)
 \le\left(\frac16+\frac{5n}{12}\right)\delta_R(t)
 +\frac5{24}\left(\frac{17}{32}\right)^n.
\]
The induced modulus obeys
\[
 \frac{7}{12}r\le\omega(r)\le2r(1+\log(1/r)),
\]
so the rate-box theorem gives a relative whole-path finitary realization.
Every off-root coordinate nevertheless has positive essential influence on
the root rate, and the summed worst-case coordinate oscillation is infinite.
Thus no finite-mean nonnegative LOCAL mixture exists.

\subsection{Mean control does not imply finite certificates}

On a two-orbit $(\mathbb{Z}\times C_2)$-set, retain a static iid environment
and let active-pair rates depend on a closed tail event with empty interior.
Conditional on the environment, independent pair clocks give a causal
equivariant strong process with diagonal-DPP marginals and satisfying
\eqref{eq:mean}.  The generator nevertheless forces a discontinuous
conditional root-endpoint kernel on a positive-measure set.  A relative
arbitrary-completion finite certificate would make an everywhere-defined
version continuous at almost every input, a contradiction.

\subsection{Failure of the box method is not failure of joint finitarity}

A single-orbit dynamic replacement has exact DPP marginals and a causal
relative strong realization.  Its finite-box widths stay bounded away from
zero on an event of positive probability, so the exact-box criterion fails
for every nested query family.  Its one-root endpoint conditional law is
nevertheless almost-everywhere continuous and that single finite-valued
output has a relative finite sampler.  This does not produce a finitary
realization of the complete joint process: independently sampling the
one-coordinate kernels would destroy the joint law.

\section{A non-diagonal DPP application}

Assume $\Gamma$ contains an element of infinite order.  A positive sandwich
construction gives a full-support convolution probability $v=a*w*a$ and a
positive contraction $V=T_v=AWA$, with $\ker V=\ker A$, where $A$ is a cyclic
spectral-zero operator.  For a weighted-small finite-support Hermitian
background $b$, set
\[
 K_t=\frac12I+T_b+\delta tV^2.
\]
This kernel curve is uniformly gapped.  Its exact DPP birth rates satisfy the
cylinder equations and admit a summable local-update decomposition after the
inverse-pair coefficients of the convolution kernel are ordered.

\begin{theorem}[Cyclic non-diagonal application]
For the preceding family, there is a total equivariant relative finitary DPP
process.  In a constructed proper left-invariant metric, both the relative
query count and query radius have finite first moments.  Moreover, if a
bounded finite-propagation operator $T$ satisfies $TT^*\preceq V^2$, then
$T=0$.
\end{theorem}

Thus the increment has no nonzero finite-propagation square minorant.  A
separate gapped initial sampler may be composed with the relative process to
obtain a joint iid finitary endpoint pair, but this composition does not
transfer the relative moment bounds.  The construction requires an
infinite-order element, a constructed increment, weighted-small backgrounds,
and a constructed metric; it is not general exact ordered joint iid sampling.

\section{Logical picture and open problems}

The proved implications can be summarized as
\[
 \text{finite range}\subsetneq\text{LOCAL}
 \subsetneq\text{BOX+WIDTH}\subseteq\text{relative finitary}
 \subseteq\text{relative strong}\subseteq\text{weak}.
\]
The first two strict containments are witnessed by the cyclic and nonlocal
rate-box examples.  The static environment separates relative strong from
relative finitary.  Necessity of BOX+WIDTH for full-process finitarity is not
known.

We close with four open problems.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
\item For arbitrary nonamenable groups, do all finite-valued Borel covariant
  DPP birth rates satisfying the integrated cylinder equations and ordinary
  weak-existence hypotheses admit an invariant weak realization?
\item Which rates outside BOX+WIDTH admit a relative finitary full-process
  realization?  The dynamic example settles only a one-output kernel.
\item Can arbitrary ordered DPP kernels be joined by one exact equivariant iid
  factor?
\item Can the cyclic application's query moments be obtained in a preassigned
  word metric or after composition with the initial sampler?
\end{enumerate}
