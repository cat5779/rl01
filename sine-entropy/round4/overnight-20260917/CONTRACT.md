## New work and evidence

Each assignment must state an exact new research question and the difference from prior work. Tiny contrast extensions (for example replacing 10^-13 by 10^-11), finite replays, generic identities, literature lists, and 'if P2 then the target' do not count as completion. Seek a substantial structural lemma, meaningful non-microscopic coverage, or a rigorous counterexample to a precisely stated live method. No scope weakening or substitution of count/spectral entropy for complete-configuration entropy. Preserve actual law, normalization, acceleration, Fisher and moving count-layer terms; value bounds are not Hessian bounds.

Work at least 90 minutes of substantive research per fresh round unless a NEW, complete, checkable substantial result is obtained. Record honest start/end times, checkpoints and remaining gaps. Never pad time or invent elapsed time. If interrupted or incomplete, say so. Do not use research success labels to justify stopping on tiny results.

The coordinator can read chat replies while the user is away but cannot obtain your ZIP. Every final reply must therefore contain a readable evidence checkpoint: exact theorem/quantifiers, definitions, key derivation and unpaid steps, explicit constants/scaling, prior-vs-new dependency table, deterministic check descriptions, and a precise next obligation. Do not hide the sole proof evidence behind an attachment. Preserve earlier artifacts and return one cumulative self-contained ZIP with Markdown, Python and receipts; no PR and no repository access is assumed.

Unreviewed chat claims may be pursued by their authors as provisional leads, but cannot be promoted to reviewed premises elsewhere. The coordinator will log small progress for later download and independent audit. A substantial candidate may receive an immediate follow-up at the next scheduled check; this is a research decision, not independent certification. No claim is merged as verified without actual inspection and appropriate reproduction.
