# Candidate and failure ledger — fresh S9 round

Frozen baseline: aaf3e86576a867067701089805c9e5e5a83e44f5. This ledger distinguishes
an actual new proof from a probe, a representation, and an unpaid bridge.
Sources and exact imported claims are in REFERENCES.md and DEPENDENCY_DELTA.md.
The initial candidate comparison was recorded before the deep proof attempt;
the entries below include its subsequent outcomes.

## A. Complete production across context/degree

Candidate: the complete original-shift production curvature, or its Jensen
gap, increases when the context and Fejer degree grow. This would allow the
accepted R3 margin to survive. Fast falsification: evaluate ALL complete atoms
and analytic jets at R3/R5, then freeze a large degree and enlarge contexts.
The tested R3/R5 sweep and degree-10000 contexts did not show a contrary
increment. Further random bipartite and general Hermitian context probes also
did not establish a counterexample. **No comparison theorem proved.**

The distinction between changing context at a fixed process and changing the
Fejer process remains essential. This route is not claimed closed or refuted.
A more general joint-matrix-convexity shortcut was falsified along a non-scalar
Hermitian direction; it cannot be substituted for common-shift convexity.

## B. Production-weighted critical-scale transport — promoted

Candidate frozen during the initial proof search: at shifts theta/sqrt(R),
the weighted exterior-count distribution converges to a translated Gaussian
mixture with the full midpoint production as its mixing weight. This would
replace a vanishing-negative-budget fantasy by an explicit signed transport.
Fast falsification tests: retain changing complete-word weights, conditional
count centers, Fejer defect and the derivative acceleration; check whether
conditioning on a fixed local word can change the count variance extensively.

Outcome: **proved scoped theorem**, Main Sections 1-8. All-word finite-rank
conditioning and local-predictor replacement supply the missing weighted
control. A complex event-determinant bound permits actual midpoint
curvature. Positive and negative order-R bands have the same explicit
constant; their full leading total is zero. General density requires a
noise-adapted count center. The next-order sign remains unpaid.

Small radii 3,5,7 do not yet show the eventual negative central-band sign.
They are not passed off as asymptotic evidence or an explicit radius bound.

## C. Spectral-score / local statistical experiment — promoted companion

Candidate: the leading complete-law score on a nearly projection spectrum is
count measurable, with a defect-controlled error. Fast test: derive both
score and atom acceleration through the FIXED spectral observation channel,
not by declaring unitary entropy invariance.

Outcome: finite L2 score and L1 acceleration bounds, full-law KL comparison
to a count tilt, and a parallel author proof of second-order compensation.
The full-law KL is O(log(R)/R) at the critical scale. This does not control an
entropy Hessian or remove spatial conditional entropy.

A possible bridge using only fixedness, count preservation and layerwise
bistochasticity was then tested. **It is rigorously false**: the growing
identity-middle/uniform-other-layers channel has positive output entropy
curvature of order n^(3/2). Its input has fixed half rank proportion and fixed
nonzero contrast. The channel is not the common exterior powers of a unitary
and does not commute with deletion. Stronger compatibility-based bridges
remain possible; the counterexample's scope is not enlarged to them.

## D. External-field and count-tilt positivity

Candidate: sign a simpler external-field production and transfer its sign to
the actual affine shift. Fast test: use a genuine four-site consecutive-Fourier
projection and retain the field path's contrast acceleration.

Outcome: **exact adverse witness** at c=19/20. Full center production has
external-field second derivative < -1/10. The missing contrast-acceleration
term explains why this is not an actual-shift counterexample. The rational
word table and logarithm inequalities are preserved. Larger Fourier probes
were diagnostics only; the four-site analytic witness is the claimed result.

## E. Bipartite geometry and fair-spin series

At half density the Fejer off-diagonal graph is bipartite, and each parity
marginal is an iid Bernoulli law. This suggests controlling the mutual
information between parities, or the balanced-minor expansion. Formal small
examples had the expected even powers and favorable coefficients. However,
the logarithmic entropy expansion contains alternating products of minors;
no all-order, volume-uniform signed bound was obtained. An operator norm bound
for a log determinant does not automatically control the differentiated
complete-word average. **Representation and probes only.**

A related layer-spatial-entropy concavity guess fails numerically already
for finite consecutive-Fourier projections (e.g. the middle layer at n=8).
No unproved layer concavity is used in the new theorem.

## F. Broader binary-channel classes

Arbitrary homogeneous half-rank input laws, complement-symmetric input laws,
and balanced-marginal input laws were probed as possible larger classes.
These finite tests did not prove a universal theorem. The repository's known
variable-cardinality channel counterexample has biased marginals; it cannot
be relabelled as a homogeneous or balanced-input counterexample. An attempted
balanced modification was also checked and did not inherit its contrary
sign. **No theorem imported or asserted for these larger classes.**

## G. Discrete displacement / entropic interpolation

Primary sources by Leonard, Hillion, and Samson were checked for a curvature
mechanism on the discrete cube. The natural DPP shift flux has BB residual
W^2(BC-AD), nonzero for a conditionally correlated pair, so the direct
BB-equality substitution fails. Hillion's tensorization theorem additionally
uses a canonical path. A different flux/reference is not ruled out, but has
not been constructed or paid. This is a hypothesis mismatch, not evidence
that all transport methods fail.

## H. Count entropy, estimation, and quantum analogies

The scalar Shepp-Olkin theorem, stationary DPP likelihood approximations,
scalar thinning/ultra-log-concavity, entropic independence, and quantum or
spectral entropy papers were screened. None supplied the specific missing
full-configuration signed estimate with its hypotheses verified. The source
ledger records what was read versus merely screened. No literature-wide
novelty or exhaustion claim is made.
