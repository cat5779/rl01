# Gap audit and proof-obligation map

## Status and target-level boundary

The status is PROVED_SCOPED_LEMMA. The complete all-density/all-offset sine target is not proved or disproved. The new finite theorem and rate corollary cover only the region expressly quantified in Theorem C. General Theorems A and B are estimates, not universal entropy-concavity claims.

## Paid obligations

**Correct entropy and parameter:** all derivatives use full configuration atoms from the actual product channel at fixed c, with common offset a. Neither H(|Y|), Tr b(K), nor sum b(K_ii) replaces H(Y).

**Posterior/reference compatibility:** Section 2 proves positive-field DPP closure for all Hermitian contractions, including projections by continuity. Gaussian prior fields include -t|X|/2 for nonhomogeneous input. Partial-output priors and full-output posteriors are not interchanged without the exact rebasing identity.

**All changing-law derivatives:** Section 1 includes p'', 2p'r', r'', and the unconditional-reference Fisher term -F. The finite experiment checker tests these at nonmidpoint offsets. Gaussian reference laws are independent of a by construction; output weights are not.

**Acceleration payment:** Sections 4-6 turn A=-sum p''logp into a signed pair log-odds sum, prove a continuum scalar bound, and rebase every pair to the same full-output posterior before summing. This controls the previously lost term. It does not assert A<=0.

**Actual Fisher:** F=D-E is exact; E=Psi-2S keeps the negative off-diagonal covariance contribution. The new bound uses I''<=kappa Psi-(kappa-1)E, not merely the positivity of F. The one-site comparison Psi<=D-F_marg is proved by conditional variance, not independent-score guessing.

**Gaussian rank/time estimate:** Section 8 uses deterministic stopping, a conditional-information chain rule, and a scalar Gaussian affinity integral. It controls the actual averaged completed remainder. No claim is made about the sign of J_T'' or Q_t''.

**Uniform continuum constants:** factor two is proved analytically for all four-cell laws with equal-output probability >=1/25, and the channel forces that condition for c^2<=23/25. The regional arithmetic is uniform in dimension, all contractions, all stated offsets, and all stated contrasts. Finite tests do not supply this uniformity.

**Rate passage:** Section 9 first integrates finite H_n'' bounds, then passes to the supplied entropy-rate limit at three points. Q_n is used directly, has diagonal rho, and is not treated as a finite projection. No limiting derivative is assumed.

## Precise unpaid steps

1. At moderate fixed density, especially rho=1/2, (kappa-1)D-kappa F_marg can be positive. For the reviewed P_(4,2) midpoint it is positive although actual H'' is negative. Thus this bound does not pay the full negative budget there. A refined averaged diagonal-uncertainty or pair-log-odds estimate is still needed.
2. At rho=.01 and c=.95 the theorem covers a in [1/80,3/80], not the outer quarters [0,1/80) and (3/80,1/20]. The finite endpoint-neighborhood baseline is not uniform in n and is not used to fill this gap.
3. The explicit negative-budget region ends at c=.959, although Theorems A/B remain valid for every c<1. No claim at c=1 uses an illegal offset derivative.
4. The O(n^-1) Jensen remainder at T_n=16log n controls R_T=I(X;Y)-I(Y;F_T) only. The main term J_T remains, with the same moderate-density payment gap. Renaming this remainder as the whole target defect would be false.
5. Prior negative pair covariance alone is insufficient for the proof: the required sign must persist under the actual partial and full positive likelihood tilts. DPP closure proves that property here. No theorem for arbitrary homogeneous laws is asserted.
6. No spatial Fourier-specific cancellation beyond constant diagonal and explicit rank is used. The family scope is nevertheless genuine growing contiguous P_(n,k) and actual Q_n, not only block products. Sharper Toeplitz correlation structure remains an unexploited possible source of further improvement.
