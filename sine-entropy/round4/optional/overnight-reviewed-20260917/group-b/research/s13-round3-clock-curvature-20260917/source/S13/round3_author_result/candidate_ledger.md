# Candidate ledger

## Candidate A — midpoint corrected-clock response

- **Question:** can a signed part of the complete corrected divergence response
  be paid after actual count averaging?
- **Fast failure gate:** if `partial_z theta` changed sign, or if the averaged
  law had no nonzero mode capable of forcing entropy production, stop.
- **Outcome:** promoted and proved.
- **New theorem:** the clock-curvature entropy-production component is at least
  `n*2D_pair(c)/(1-c^2)+o(n)`.
- **Why substantive:** it is a growing-family, actual-count-weighted,
  linear-scale signed term with a closed constant, not a finite replay or formal
  Fisher decomposition.

## Candidate B — averaged two-mode random-clock obstruction

- **Question:** can the old degree-one/degree-two Jensen no-go be upgraded to
  the averaged conditional law?
- **Fast failure gate:** check the averaged degree-one amplitude.
- **Outcome:** rejected immediately; that amplitude is zero.
- **Scope repair:** the old theorem remains conditional/operator only when
  degree-one multiplier matching is separately imposed. No averaged-law no-go
  is claimed without a second nonzero averaged mode and an incompatible moment

- **Question:** does `W_n=sum pi''K_l` have an adverse `n^(3/2)` central cusp?
- **Fast evidence:** exact finite enumeration through `n=16` and local layer
  slopes.
- **Outcome:** retained but not promoted. The sign pattern is strong, yet a
  growing proof would require uniform control of all layer second differences,
  not only the center.
