## Source versus new derivations

The paper's integer representation and the atom-law identities supplied by the user are not new contributions. The bundle independently proves the algebra needed to check conventions and normalization. The reverse-Jensen estimate, its explicit extensive Fourier specialization, and the real-q product-tangent error connection are the delivered derivations.

The mathematical tools used for the new derivations are proved directly: determinant expansion; Schur complements for complementary principal minors; the Gram-Schmidt determinant bound; classical log-sum/KL nonnegativity; Cauchy-Schwarz/Hölder; and a positive-series logarithm remainder. No external hypothesis beyond those explicitly stated in proof.md is silently imported.
